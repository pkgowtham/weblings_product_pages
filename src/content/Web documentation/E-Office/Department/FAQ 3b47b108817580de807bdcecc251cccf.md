# FAQ

## 1. Why am I seeing a **500 Internal Server Error** while opening the Department page?

A **500 Internal Server Error** usually means the server encountered an unexpected problem while processing your request.

### Possible Causes

- The server is temporarily unavailable.
- A backend service is not responding.
- The database connection failed.
- The application is currently under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Check your internet connection.
- If the problem continues, contact your system administrator or support team and provide the time the error occurred.

---

## 2. Why is the Department list not loading?

If the Department table remains empty or continues loading, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- The selected Business Unit Location has no departments.
- The server is taking longer than expected to respond.
- A temporary network interruption occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Verify that your internet connection is working properly.
- Confirm that you are signed in to the correct Business Unit Location.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- If the issue persists, contact your system administrator.

---

## 3. Why can't I create a new department?

If clicking **Submit** does not create a department, the request may not have passed validation.

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- A department with the same name already exists.
- A Department Head has not been selected.
- You do not have permission to create departments.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Enter a unique department name.
- Select a Department Head.
- Verify that you have permission to create departments.
- Contact your administrator if the problem continues.

---

## 4. Why is the Department Head dropdown empty?

The **Department Head** dropdown displays only employees who are eligible to manage departments.

### Possible Causes

- No employees have been added to the selected Business Unit.
- Employee records are inactive.
- You do not have permission to view employee information.
- Employee information is still loading.

### What You Can Do

- Wait a few moments for the employee list to load.
- Refresh the page.
- Verify that employees have been created for the selected Business Unit.
- Contact your administrator if no employees are available to select.

---

## 5. Why am I getting a **403 Access Denied** or **Unauthorized** message?

This message indicates that your account does not have permission to access or modify the Department module.

### Possible Causes

- Your assigned role does not include Department permissions.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access to the Department module has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Ensure you are using the correct user account.
- Contact your system administrator to request the necessary permissions.
