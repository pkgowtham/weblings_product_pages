# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a new client?

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- Client Name already exists.
- Client Code already exists.
- Billing Currency or Billing Type has not been selected.
- Client Manager has not been selected.
- You do not have permission to create clients.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Enter a unique Client Name.
- Use a unique Client Code.
- Select valid Billing Currency and Billing Type.
- Select a Client Manager.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why am I getting a "Duplicate Client Code" error?

### Possible Causes

- Another client already uses the same Client Code.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a unique Client Code.
- Search the client list before creating a new client.

---

## 3. Why is the Client Manager dropdown empty?

### Possible Causes

- No employees have been assigned as Client Managers.
- Employee information is still loading.
- You do not have permission to view employees.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait for the employee list to load.
- Contact your administrator.

---

## 4. Why can't I edit a client?

### Possible Causes

- You do not have edit permission.
- Your login session has expired.
- The client record is locked by another process.

### What You Can Do

- Sign in again.
- Verify your permissions.
- Try again after a few minutes.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 5. Why can't I delete a client?

### Possible Causes

- The client is linked to one or more active projects.
- The client has unpaid invoices.
- The client is associated with active contracts.
- You do not have permission to delete clients.

### What You Can Do

- Complete or reassign active projects.
- Resolve outstanding invoices.
- Verify your permissions.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is required.

---

## 6. Why is the Client list not loading?

### Possible Causes

- Slow internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.
- Network interruption.
- No clients have been created.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue persists.

---

## 7. Why can't I search for a client?

### Possible Causes

- The entered Client Name or Client Code is incorrect.
- The client has been deleted.
- No matching client exists.

### What You Can Do

- Check the spelling.
- Search using the Client Code.
- Search using the registered email address.
- Clear the search field to display all clients.

---

## 8. Why am I getting a "500 Internal Server Error"?

### Possible Causes

- The server encountered an unexpected problem.
- Backend services are temporarily unavailable.
- The application is under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Try again after a few minutes.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue persists.

---

## 9. Why am I getting a "403 Access Denied" or "Unauthorized" message?

### Possible Causes

- Your role does not have permission to manage clients.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your assigned permissions.
- Contact your administrator if necessary.

---

## 10. Why aren't my changes visible after updating a client?

### Possible Causes

- The update was not completed successfully.
- The page has not refreshed.
- Temporary synchronization delay.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Verify that the update completed successfully.
- Update the client again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.
