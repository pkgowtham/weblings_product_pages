# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a Billing Type?

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The Billing Type Name already exists.
- The Custom Code already exists.
- You do not have permission to create billing types.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Enter a unique Billing Type Name.
- Use a unique Custom Code.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why am I getting a "Duplicate Custom Code" error?

### Possible Causes

- Another billing type already uses the same Custom Code.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a different Custom Code.
- Search the billing type list to verify existing records.

---

## 3. Why can't I edit a Billing Type?

### Possible Causes

- Your account does not have edit permission.
- Your login session has expired.
- Another process currently locks the billing type.

### What You Can Do

- Sign in again.
- Verify your permissions.
- Try again after a few minutes.
- Contact your administrator if the issue persists.

---

## 4. Why can't I delete a Billing Type?

### Possible Causes

- The billing type is assigned to one or more clients.
- The billing type is linked to active projects.
- You do not have permission to delete billing types.

### What You Can Do

- Remove or reassign the billing type from associated clients or projects.
- Verify your permissions.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is required.

---

## 5. Why isn't the Billing Type list loading?

### Possible Causes

- Slow internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.
- Network interruption.
- No billing types have been created.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 6. Why can't I search for a Billing Type?

### Possible Causes

- The Billing Type Name or Custom Code is incorrect.
- No matching billing type exists.
- The billing type has been deleted.

### What You Can Do

- Check the spelling.
- Search using the Custom Code.
- Clear the search field to display all billing types.

---

## 7. Why am I getting a "500 Internal Server Error"?

### Possible Causes

- The server encountered an unexpected problem.
- Backend services are temporarily unavailable.
- The application is under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Try again after a few minutes.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue persists.

---

## 8. Why am I getting a "403 Access Denied" or "Unauthorized" message?

### Possible Causes

- Your role does not have permission to manage billing types.
- Your login session has expired.
- Your administrator has restricted access.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your assigned permissions.
- Contact your administrator if necessary.

---

## 9. Why aren't my changes visible after updating a Billing Type?

### Possible Causes

- The update was not completed successfully.
- The page has not refreshed.
- There is a temporary synchronization delay.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Verify that the update completed successfully.
- Update the billing type again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.
