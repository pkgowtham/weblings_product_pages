# FAQ

### I can't create a team.

**Possible causes:**

- Required fields are incomplete.
- A project has not been selected.
- A team lead has not been selected.

**Solution:**

- Complete all required fields.
- Select a project.
- Select a team lead.
- Click **Create Team** again.

---

### The Create Team button is disabled.

**Possible cause:**

Required information has not been entered.

**Solution:**

- Complete all mandatory fields in the Create Team modal.
- Once the required fields are completed, the **Create Team** button will be enabled.

---

### I can't find an employee while adding members.

**Possible causes:**

- The employee does not exist.
- The employee is inactive.

**Solution:**

- Verify the employee has been added to the organization.
- Check that the employee is active.
- Use the search bar to search by employee name.

---

### The selected members are not added.

**Possible cause:**

The **Add New Members** button was not clicked.

**Solution:**

- Select the required employees.
- Click **Add New Members** to confirm the selection.
- Verify that the member count is displayed on the **Add Members** button.

---

### I can't edit or delete a team.

**Possible cause:**

You do not have sufficient permissions.

**Solution:**

Contact your system administrator to verify your access rights.

---

### I accidentally deleted a team.

**Possible cause:**

The deletion was confirmed.

**Solution:**

Deleted teams cannot be restored from the Teams screen. Contact your system administrator if the team needs to be recreated.
