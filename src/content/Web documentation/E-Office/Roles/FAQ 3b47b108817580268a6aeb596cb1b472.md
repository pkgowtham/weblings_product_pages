# FAQ

## 1. Why am I seeing a **500 Internal Server Error** while opening the Roles & Permissions page?

A **500 Internal Server Error** usually means the server encountered an unexpected problem while processing your request.

### Possible Causes

- The server is temporarily unavailable.
- A backend service responsible for loading roles is not responding.
- The database connection failed.
- The application is currently under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Check your internet connection.
- If the problem continues, contact your system administrator or support team and provide the time the error occurred.

---

## 2. Why is the Roles list not loading?

If the Roles table remains empty or continues loading, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- No roles have been created yet.
- The server is taking longer than expected to respond.
- A temporary network interruption occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Verify that your internet connection is working properly.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- If the issue persists, contact your system administrator.

---

## 3. Why can't I create a new role?

If clicking **Create** does not create a role, the request may not have passed validation.

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The role name or custom code already exists.
- No permissions have been selected.
- You do not have permission to create roles.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Enter a unique role name and custom code.
- Select the required permissions.
- Verify that you have permission to create roles.
- Contact your administrator if the problem continues.

---

## 4. Why can't I select or assign permissions?

If the permission list is empty or you cannot select permissions, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- Permission data has not finished loading.
- Your account does not have permission to manage roles.
- A temporary server or network issue occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few moments for the permissions to load.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Contact your administrator if permissions are still unavailable.

---

## 5. Why am I getting a **403 Access Denied** or **Unauthorized** message?

This message indicates that your account does not have permission to access or manage Roles & Permissions.

### Possible Causes

- Your assigned role does not include Role Management permissions.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Ensure you are using the correct user account.
- Contact your system administrator to request the necessary permissions.

---

## 6. Why are my role changes not being saved?

If your changes are not saved after clicking **Save Changes**, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are incomplete.
- The internet connection was interrupted.
- Your session has expired.
- The server rejected the update request.

### What You Can Do

- Verify that all required fields are completed.
- Check your internet connection.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Sign in again if your session has expired.

---

## 7. Why can't I delete a role?

If the **Delete** button is unavailable or the role cannot be deleted, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- The role is currently assigned to one or more users.
- Your account does not have permission to delete roles.
- The role is protected by organizational policies.

### What You Can Do

- Remove or reassign users from the role before deleting it.
- Verify that you have Delete permission.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 8. Why is the Search box not showing the role I am looking for?

If you cannot find a role using the Search box, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- The role name or custom code was entered incorrectly.
- The role has not been created yet.
- The role has already been deleted.

### What You Can Do

- Check the spelling of the role name or custom code.
- Clear the search field and try again.
- Refresh the page to load the latest role list.

---

## 9. Why am I seeing a **Network Error** message?

A Network Error means the application cannot communicate with the server.

### Possible Causes

- Your internet connection is unstable.
- The server is temporarily unavailable.
- Network connectivity was interrupted.

### What You Can Do

- Check your internet connection.
- Refresh the page.
- Try again after a few minutes.
- Contact your IT administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 10. Why was I automatically logged out while managing roles?

For security reasons, your session may expire after a period of inactivity.

### Possible Causes

- Your session timed out.
- Your account was signed in from another device.
- The system automatically ended your session.

### What You Can Do

- Sign in again.
- Save your work regularly.
- Contact your administrator if the issue occurs frequently.
