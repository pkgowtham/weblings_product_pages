# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a new role?

### Possible Causes

- The Role Name is empty.
- A role with the same name already exists at the same level.
- You do not have permission to create roles.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a Role Name.
- Use a unique role name.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why isn't the Add (+) icon visible?

### Possible Causes

- You do not have permission to modify the hierarchy.
- The hierarchy is currently loading.
- The selected role cannot have child roles.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Verify your permissions.
- Contact your administrator if the icon is unavailable.

---

## 3. Why can't I edit a role?

### Possible Causes

- Your account does not have edit permission.
- The role is locked by your administrator.
- Your login session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Contact your administrator to request edit access.

---

## 4. Why can't I delete a role?

### Possible Causes

- Employees are still assigned to the role.
- Child roles exist under the selected role.
- A redistribution strategy has not been selected.
- You do not have delete permission.

### What You Can Do

- Reassign employees.
- Move or delete child roles first.
- Select a redistribution strategy.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is restricted.

---

## 5. Why is the Redistribution Strategy required?

### Possible Causes

- The selected role currently has employees assigned to it.

### What You Can Do

- Choose **Move All to Superior Role**.
- Or select **Assign New Role First** before deleting the role.

---

## 6. Why can't I select a superior role?

### Possible Causes

- The selected role is already at the highest hierarchy level.
- No eligible parent role exists.
- The hierarchy data has not finished loading.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait until the hierarchy loads completely.
- Create a suitable parent role if required.

---

## 7. Why isn't the Organization Tree displaying correctly?

### Possible Causes

- The hierarchy data is still loading.
- No hierarchy has been created.
- A temporary server issue occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Create roles first.
- Wait a few moments and try again.

---

## 8. Why am I getting a **500 Internal Server Error**?

### Possible Causes

- The server encountered an unexpected problem.
- A backend service is temporarily unavailable.
- The application is under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 9. Why am I getting a **403 Access Denied** or **Unauthorized** message?

### Possible Causes

- Your account does not have permission to manage role hierarchies.
- Your session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your assigned permissions.
- Contact your administrator if access is required.

---

## 10. Why aren't my hierarchy changes visible?

### Possible Causes

- The update has not been saved.
- Cached information is being displayed.
- A synchronization delay occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the hierarchy page.
- Verify the operation completed successfully.
- Wait a few moments and check again.
