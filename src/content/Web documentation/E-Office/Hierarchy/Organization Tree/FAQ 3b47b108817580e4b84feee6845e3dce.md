# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I add a Level 1 employee?

### Possible Causes

- No Level 1 role has been created.
- No employees are available for assignment.
- You do not have permission to manage the organization tree.

### What You Can Do

- Create a Level 1 role in the **Role Hierarchy** module.
- Add employees to the system.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why is the Role dropdown empty?

### Possible Causes

- No roles have been created in the Role Hierarchy.
- The hierarchy is still loading.
- You do not have permission to view roles.

### What You Can Do

- Create roles in the **Role Hierarchy** module.
- Refresh the page.
- Wait for the hierarchy to load completely.
- Contact your administrator if the issue persists.

---

## 3. Why is the Member dropdown empty?

### Possible Causes

- No employees are available.
- All employees have already been assigned.
- No employees are eligible for the selected role.

### What You Can Do

- Create employee records.
- Verify employee eligibility.
- Refresh the page and try again.

---

## 4. Why can't I add a child employee?

### Possible Causes

- No child roles exist under the selected role.
- The selected role is the lowest level in the hierarchy.
- The hierarchy has not finished loading.

### What You Can Do

- Create additional child roles in the **Role Hierarchy** module.
- Refresh the page.
- Wait until the hierarchy loads completely.

---

## 5. Why can't I edit an employee assignment?

### Possible Causes

- You do not have edit permission.
- The employee assignment is locked.
- Your login session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Contact your administrator for edit permissions.

---

## 6. Why can't I delete an employee?

### Possible Causes

- The employee has direct reports.
- A redistribution strategy has not been selected.
- You do not have delete permission.

### What You Can Do

- Choose one of the available redistribution strategies.
- Reassign employees before deletion.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is restricted.

---

## 7. Why can't I select another manager?

### Possible Causes

- No eligible managers exist.
- The selected employee is already assigned.
- Manager data is still loading.

### What You Can Do

- Create another manager in the organization tree.
- Refresh the page.
- Wait until the manager list loads completely.

---

## 8. Why isn't the Organization Tree displaying correctly?

### Possible Causes

- The organization tree is still loading.
- No employees have been assigned.
- A temporary server issue occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Add employees to the organization tree.
- Wait a few moments and try again.

---

## 9. Why am I getting a **500 Internal Server Error**?

### Possible Causes

- The server encountered an unexpected problem.
- A backend service is temporarily unavailable.
- The application is under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 10. Why am I getting a **403 Access Denied** or **Unauthorized** message?

### Possible Causes

- Your account does not have permission to manage the Organization Tree.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your assigned permissions.
- Contact your administrator if access is required.
