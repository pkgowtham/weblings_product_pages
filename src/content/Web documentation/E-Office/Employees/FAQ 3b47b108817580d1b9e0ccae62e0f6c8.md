# FAQ

## 1. Why am I seeing a **500 Internal Server Error** while opening the Employees page?

A **500 Internal Server Error** usually indicates that the server encountered an unexpected problem while processing your request.

### Possible Causes

- The server is temporarily unavailable.
- A backend service is not responding.
- The database connection failed.
- The application is currently under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Check your internet connection.
- If the problem continues, contact your system administrator or support team and provide the time the error occurred.

---

## 2. Why can't I create a new employee?

If clicking **Finish** does not create the employee, the request may not have passed validation.

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The organization email address is already in use.
- The email address has not been verified using the **Check** button.
- A required dropdown value has not been selected.
- You do not have permission to create employees.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Verify that the organization email is available.
- Select all mandatory dropdown values.
- Ensure the password meets the required security rules.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 3. Why can't I create the organization email?

The organization email must be unique before it can be assigned to an employee.

### Possible Causes

- The email address is already being used.
- The email format is invalid.
- The selected email domain is unavailable.
- The server could not validate the email address.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a different email address.
- Verify that the email format is correct.
- Click **Check** again to verify availability.
- Refresh the page and try again.

---

## 4. Why is the Reporting Manager dropdown empty?

The Reporting Manager list only displays eligible employees.

### Possible Causes

- No managers have been created.
- No employees exist in the selected Business Unit or Location.
- Employee information is still loading.
- You do not have permission to view employee records.

### What You Can Do

- Wait a few moments for the list to load.
- Refresh the page.
- Verify that managers have been created.
- Contact your administrator if no managers are available.

---

## 5. Why can't I select a Department, Shift, or Worker Type?

Some dropdown lists depend on previously selected information.

### Possible Causes

- A Business Unit has not been selected.
- A Location has not been selected.
- Required master data has not yet been created.
- The selected Business Unit has no available records.

### What You Can Do

- Select a Business Unit first.
- Choose a valid Location.
- Verify that Departments, Shifts, Worker Types, and other required records have already been created.
- Contact your administrator if the lists remain empty.

---

## 6. Why am I getting a **403 Access Denied** or **Unauthorized** message?

This message indicates that your account does not have permission to access or manage employee records.

### Possible Causes

- Your assigned role does not include Employee Management permissions.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Ensure you are using the correct account.
- Contact your administrator to request the necessary permissions.

---

## 7. Why are my employee details not being saved?

If your changes are not saved after clicking **Save Changes**, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- Required information is missing.
- The internet connection was interrupted.
- Your session has expired.
- The server rejected the update request.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Verify your internet connection.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Sign in again if your session has expired.

---

## 8. Why can't I delete an employee?

If the employee cannot be deleted, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- The employee is linked to active projects or teams.
- The employee has pending approvals or assigned tasks.
- Your account does not have permission to delete employees.

### What You Can Do

- Complete or reassign any pending work.
- Verify that you have Delete permission.
- Contact your administrator if the employee still cannot be deleted.

---

## 9. Why is the employee list not loading?

If the employee table remains empty or continues loading, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.
- No employees have been created.
- A network interruption occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the problem continues.

---

## 10. Why am I seeing a **Network Error** message?

A Network Error means the application cannot communicate with the server.

### Possible Causes

- Your internet connection is unstable.
- The server is temporarily unavailable.
- A firewall, VPN, or proxy is blocking the connection.

### What You Can Do

- Check your internet connection.
- Refresh the page.
- Disable your VPN or proxy if applicable.
- Contact your IT administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 11. Why was I automatically logged out?

For security reasons, your session may expire after a period of inactivity.

### Possible Causes

- Your session timed out.
- Your account was signed in from another device.
- The system automatically ended your session.

### What You Can Do

- Sign in again.
- Save your work regularly while creating or editing employees.
- Contact your administrator if the issue occurs frequently.

---

## 12. Why can't I find an employee using Search?

If Search does not return the expected employee, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- The employee name or email was entered incorrectly.
- The employee belongs to another Business Unit.
- The employee record has been deleted.

### What You Can Do

- Check the spelling of the employee's name or email.
- Clear the search field and try again.
- Refresh the page to load the latest employee records.
