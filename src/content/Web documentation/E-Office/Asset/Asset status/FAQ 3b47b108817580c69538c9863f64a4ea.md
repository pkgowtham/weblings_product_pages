# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a new Asset Status?

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- An asset status with the same name already exists.
- You do not have permission to create asset statuses.
- A temporary server issue occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Use a unique asset status name.
- Verify your permissions.
- Refresh the page and try again.

---

## 2. Why am I getting a "Duplicate Asset Status" error?

### Possible Causes

- Another asset status already uses the same name.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a different asset status name.
- Search the Asset Status list before creating a new record.

---

## 3. Why can't I update an Asset Status?

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are empty.
- The updated name already exists.
- You do not have permission to edit asset statuses.
- Your session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all mandatory fields.
- Use a unique name.
- Sign in again if your session has expired.
- Contact your administrator if editing is restricted.

---

## 4. Why can't I delete an Asset Status?

### Possible Causes

- The asset status is currently assigned to one or more assets.
- You do not have permission to delete asset statuses.
- The asset status is protected by organizational policies.

### What You Can Do

- Assign another status to the affected assets first.
- Verify your permissions.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is required.

---

## 5. Why is the Asset Status list not loading?

### Possible Causes

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.
- Network interruption.
- No asset statuses have been created yet.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Wait a few moments and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 6. Why am I getting a "500 Internal Server Error"?

### Possible Causes

- The server encountered an unexpected error.
- Backend services are temporarily unavailable.
- The application is under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Try again after a few minutes.
- Contact your system administrator if the problem persists.

---

## 7. Why am I getting a "403 Access Denied" or "Unauthorized" message?

### Possible Causes

- Your role does not include Asset Status permissions.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your assigned permissions.
- Contact your administrator if access is required.

---

## 8. Why are my changes not visible after updating?

### Possible Causes

- The update request was unsuccessful.
- The page has not refreshed.
- A temporary synchronization delay occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the Asset Status list.
- Verify that the update was successful.
- Try updating the record again.

---

## 9. Why can't I find an Asset Status using Search?

### Possible Causes

- The entered name is incorrect.
- The asset status has been renamed or deleted.
- No matching records exist.

### What You Can Do

- Check the spelling of the asset status name.
- Search using partial keywords.
- Clear the search box to display all records.

---

## 10. Why can't I save the Asset Status after entering all details?

### Possible Causes

- Invalid characters were entered in the name.
- Validation rules were not met.
- A temporary server issue occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Review all entered information.
- Use a valid and unique asset status name.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue persists.
