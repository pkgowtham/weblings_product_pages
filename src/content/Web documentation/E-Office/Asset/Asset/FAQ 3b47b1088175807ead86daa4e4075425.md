# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a new Asset?

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- Asset Category or Asset Type has not been selected.
- Asset ID or Custom Code already exists.
- Asset Condition or Asset Status has not been selected.
- You do not have permission to create assets.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Select valid Asset Category and Asset Type.
- Use a unique Asset ID and Custom Code.
- Select Asset Condition and Asset Status.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why am I getting a "Duplicate Asset ID" or "Duplicate Custom Code" error?

### Possible Causes

- Another asset already uses the same Asset ID.
- Another asset already uses the same Custom Code.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a unique Asset ID.
- Use a different Custom Code.
- Search the Asset list before creating a new asset.

---

## 3. Why can't I upload an Asset Image?

### Possible Causes

- The selected file format is not supported.
- The image exceeds the maximum allowed file size.
- The upload was interrupted due to a network issue.

### What You Can Do

- Upload a supported image format (JPG, JPEG, PNG).
- Reduce the image size if necessary.
- Check your internet connection and upload again.

---

## 4. Why can't I update an Asset?

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are incomplete.
- The updated Asset ID or Custom Code already exists.
- You do not have permission to edit assets.
- Your login session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all mandatory fields.
- Use unique Asset ID and Custom Code values.
- Sign in again if your session has expired.
- Contact your administrator if editing is restricted.

---

## 5. Why can't I delete an Asset?

### Possible Causes

- The asset is currently assigned to an employee.
- The asset is linked to an active asset assignment.
- The asset is referenced by another module.
- You do not have permission to delete assets.

### What You Can Do

- Unassign the asset before deleting it.
- Complete any pending asset return process.
- Verify your permissions.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is required.

---

## 6. Why is the Asset list not loading?

### Possible Causes

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.
- Network interruption.
- No assets have been created yet.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Wait a few moments and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 7. Why am I getting a "500 Internal Server Error"?

### Possible Causes

- The server encountered an unexpected error.
- Backend services are temporarily unavailable.
- The application is currently under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Try again after a few minutes.
- Contact your system administrator if the problem persists.

---

## 8. Why am I getting a "403 Access Denied" or "Unauthorized" message?

### Possible Causes

- Your role does not have permission to manage assets.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your assigned permissions.
- Contact your administrator if access is required.

---

## 9. Why can't I find an Asset using Search?

### Possible Causes

- The Asset Name, Asset ID, or Custom Code entered is incorrect.
- The asset has been renamed or deleted.
- No matching records exist.

### What You Can Do

- Check the spelling of your search keyword.
- Search using part of the Asset Name.
- Search using the Asset ID or Custom Code.
- Clear the search box to display all assets.

---

## 10. Why aren't my changes visible after updating an Asset?

### Possible Causes

- The update request was unsuccessful.
- The page has not refreshed.
- A temporary synchronization delay occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the Asset list.
- Verify that the update completed successfully.
- Try updating the asset again.
- Contact your administrator if the problem continues.
