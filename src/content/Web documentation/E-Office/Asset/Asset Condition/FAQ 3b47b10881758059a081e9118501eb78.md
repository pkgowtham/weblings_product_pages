# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a new Asset Condition?

### Possible Causes

- The Name field is empty.
- An asset condition with the same name already exists.
- You do not have permission to create asset conditions.
- The request could not be processed due to a temporary server issue.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a valid asset condition name.
- Use a unique name.
- Verify that you have the required permissions.
- Refresh the page and try again.

---

## 2. Why am I getting a "Duplicate Asset Condition" error?

### Possible Causes

- Another asset condition already uses the same name.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a different asset condition name.
- Search the Asset Condition list to check for existing records before creating a new one.

---

## 3. Why can't I update an Asset Condition?

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are empty.
- You do not have edit permissions.
- The asset condition has been modified by another user.
- Your session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Sign in again if your session has expired.
- Contact your administrator if editing remains unavailable.

---

## 4. Why can't I delete an Asset Condition?

### Possible Causes

- The asset condition is currently assigned to one or more assets.
- You do not have permission to delete asset conditions.
- The asset condition is protected by your organization's asset policy.

### What You Can Do

- Remove or update the asset assignments first.
- Verify your permissions.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is required.

---

## 5. Why is the Asset Condition list not loading?

### Possible Causes

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.
- Network interruption.
- No asset conditions have been created yet.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Wait a few moments and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 6. Why am I getting a "500 Internal Server Error"?

### Possible Causes

- The server encountered an unexpected error.
- A backend service is temporarily unavailable.
- Scheduled maintenance is in progress.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Try again after a few minutes.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue persists.

---

## 7. Why am I getting a "403 Access Denied" or "Unauthorized" message?

### Possible Causes

- Your role does not include Asset Condition permissions.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your assigned permissions.
- Contact your administrator if access is required.

---

## 8. Why are my changes not appearing after clicking Update?

### Possible Causes

- The update request was unsuccessful.
- A temporary synchronization delay occurred.
- The page has not refreshed.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the Asset Condition list.
- Verify that the update was successful.
- Try updating the record again.

---

## 9. Why can't I find an Asset Condition using Search?

### Possible Causes

- The search keyword is incorrect.
- The asset condition has been renamed or deleted.
- No matching records exist.

### What You Can Do

- Check the spelling of the asset condition name.
- Search using partial keywords.
- Clear the search box to display all records.

---

## 10. Why can't I save an Asset Condition after entering all details?

### Possible Causes

- The entered name contains invalid characters.
- The record violates validation rules.
- A temporary server issue occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Review all entered information.
- Use a valid and unique name.
- Try again after refreshing the page.
