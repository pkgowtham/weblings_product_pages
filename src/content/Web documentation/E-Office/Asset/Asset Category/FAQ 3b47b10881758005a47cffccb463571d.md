# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a new Asset Category?

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The Name or Custom Code already exists.
- You do not have permission to create asset categories.
- A temporary server issue occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Enter a unique Name and Custom Code.
- Verify your permissions.
- Refresh the page and try again.

---

## 2. Why am I getting a "Duplicate Asset Category" error?

### Possible Causes

- Another asset category already uses the same Name.
- Another asset category already uses the same Custom Code.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a unique Name.
- Use a different Custom Code.
- Search the Asset Category list before creating a new category.

---

## 3. Why can't I update an Asset Category?

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are missing.
- The updated Custom Code already exists.
- You do not have edit permission.
- Your login session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all mandatory fields.
- Use a unique Custom Code.
- Sign in again if necessary.
- Contact your administrator if editing is restricted.

---

## 4. Why can't I delete an Asset Category?

### Possible Causes

- The asset category is assigned to one or more assets.
- You do not have delete permission.
- The category is protected by organizational policies.

### What You Can Do

- Reassign the assets to another category.
- Verify your permissions.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is required.

---

## 5. Why is the Asset Category list not loading?

### Possible Causes

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.
- Network interruption.
- No asset categories have been created yet.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Verify your internet connection.
- Wait a few moments and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the problem continues.

---

## 6. Why am I getting a "500 Internal Server Error"?

### Possible Causes

- An unexpected server error occurred.
- Backend services are temporarily unavailable.
- The application is under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few minutes before trying again.
- Contact your system administrator if the error continues.

---

## 7. Why am I getting a "403 Access Denied" or "Unauthorized" message?

### Possible Causes

- Your role does not have Asset Category permissions.
- Your session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your assigned permissions.
- Contact your administrator if access is required.

---

## 8. Why are my changes not visible after updating?

### Possible Causes

- The update was not saved successfully.
- The page has not refreshed.
- A synchronization delay occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the Asset Category list.
- Verify the update was successful.
- Try updating the record again.

---

## 9. Why can't I find an Asset Category using Search?

### Possible Causes

- The entered Name or Custom Code is incorrect.
- The category has been renamed or deleted.
- No matching records exist.

### What You Can Do

- Check your search keyword.
- Search using part of the Name or Custom Code.
- Clear the search field to display all categories.

---

## 10. Why can't I save the Asset Category after entering all details?

### Possible Causes

- Invalid characters were entered in the Name or Custom Code.
- Validation rules were not met.
- A temporary server issue occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Review all entered information.
- Use valid values for the Name and Custom Code.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue persists.
