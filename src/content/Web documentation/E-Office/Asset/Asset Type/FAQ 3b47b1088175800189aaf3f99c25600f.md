# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a new Asset Type?

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The Asset Type Name already exists.
- The Custom Code already exists.
- The Asset ID Series already exists.
- You do not have permission to create asset types.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Enter a unique Name.
- Use a unique Custom Code.
- Use a unique Asset ID Series.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why am I getting a "Duplicate Asset Type" error?

### Possible Causes

- Another asset type already uses the same Name.
- The Custom Code is already in use.
- The Asset ID Series has already been assigned to another asset type.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a different Name.
- Use a unique Custom Code.
- Specify a different Asset ID Series.

---

## 3. Why can't I update an Asset Type?

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are incomplete.
- The updated Custom Code already exists.
- The updated Asset ID Series is already assigned to another asset type.
- You do not have permission to edit asset types.
- Your login session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Use unique values for the Custom Code and Asset ID Series.
- Sign in again if your session has expired.
- Contact your administrator if editing is restricted.

---

## 4. Why can't I delete an Asset Type?

### Possible Causes

- The asset type is currently assigned to one or more assets.
- You do not have permission to delete asset types.
- The asset type is protected by organizational policies.

### What You Can Do

- Reassign the assets to another asset type before deleting.
- Verify your permissions.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is required.

---

## 5. Why is the Asset Type list not loading?

### Possible Causes

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.
- Network interruption.
- No asset types have been created yet.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Verify your internet connection.
- Wait a few moments and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 6. Why am I getting a "500 Internal Server Error"?

### Possible Causes

- The server encountered an unexpected error.
- Backend services are temporarily unavailable.
- The application is under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few minutes before trying again.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue persists.

---

## 7. Why am I getting a "403 Access Denied" or "Unauthorized" message?

### Possible Causes

- Your role does not include Asset Type management permissions.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your assigned permissions.
- Contact your administrator if access is required.

---

## 8. Why are my changes not visible after updating?

### Possible Causes

- The update request was unsuccessful.
- A synchronization delay occurred.
- The page has not refreshed.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the Asset Type list.
- Verify that the update completed successfully.
- Try updating the record again.

---

## 9. Why can't I find an Asset Type using Search?

### Possible Causes

- The entered Name or Custom Code is incorrect.
- The asset type has been renamed or deleted.
- No matching records exist.

### What You Can Do

- Check the spelling of the Name or Custom Code.
- Search using partial keywords.
- Clear the search box to display all asset types.

---

## 10. Why can't I save the Asset Type after entering all details?

### Possible Causes

- Invalid characters were entered in one or more fields.
- Validation rules were not satisfied.
- A temporary server issue occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Review all entered information.
- Ensure the Name, Custom Code, and Asset ID Series are valid and unique.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue persists.
