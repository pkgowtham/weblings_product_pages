# FAQ

## 1. Why am I seeing a **500 Internal Server Error** while opening the Worker Type Management page?

A **500 Internal Server Error** usually indicates that the server encountered an unexpected problem while processing your request.

### Possible Causes

- The server is temporarily unavailable.
- A backend service responsible for loading worker types is not responding.
- The database connection failed.
- The application is currently under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Check your internet connection.
- If the problem continues, contact your system administrator or support team and provide the time the error occurred.

---

## 2. Why can't I create a new Worker Type?

If clicking **Submit** does not create the worker type, the request may not have passed validation.

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The Worker Type name already exists.
- The Custom Code is already in use.
- You do not have permission to create worker types.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Enter a unique Worker Type name.
- Use a unique Custom Code.
- Contact your administrator if the problem continues.

---

## 3. Why are my changes not being saved?

If your changes are not saved after clicking **Update**, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are incomplete.
- Your internet connection was interrupted.
- Your session has expired.
- The server rejected the update request.

### What You Can Do

- Verify that all required information has been entered.
- Check your internet connection.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Sign in again if your session has expired.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 4. Why can't I delete a Worker Type?

Some worker types cannot be deleted if they are currently assigned to employees.

### Possible Causes

- One or more employees are assigned to the Worker Type.
- The Worker Type is currently being used by active employee records.
- Your account does not have permission to delete worker types.

### What You Can Do

- Reassign employees to another Worker Type before deleting.
- Verify that the Worker Type is no longer in use.
- Contact your administrator if you require additional permissions.

---

## 5. Why am I getting a **403 Access Denied** or **Unauthorized** message?

This message indicates that your account does not have permission to access or manage Worker Types.

### Possible Causes

- Your assigned role does not include Worker Type Management permissions.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Ensure you are using the correct user account.
- Contact your system administrator to request the necessary permissions.

---

## 6. Why is the Worker Type list not loading?

If the Worker Type table remains empty or continues loading, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- No worker types have been created.
- The server is taking longer than expected to respond.
- A temporary network interruption occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Verify that your internet connection is working properly.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue persists.

---

## 7. Why can't I find a Worker Type using Search?

If the search does not return the expected result, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- The Worker Type name or Custom Code was entered incorrectly.
- The Worker Type has been deleted.
- The Worker Type has not been created yet.

### What You Can Do

- Check the spelling of the Worker Type name or Custom Code.
- Clear the search box and try again.
- Refresh the page to load the latest Worker Type records.

---

## 8. Why am I seeing a **Network Error** message?

A Network Error means the application cannot communicate with the server.

### Possible Causes

- Your internet connection is unstable.
- The server is temporarily unavailable.
- A firewall, VPN, or proxy is blocking the connection.

### What You Can Do

- Check your internet connection.
- Refresh the page.
- Disable your VPN or proxy if applicable.
- Contact your IT administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 9. Why was I automatically logged out?

For security reasons, your session may expire after a period of inactivity.

### Possible Causes

- Your session timed out.
- Your account was signed in from another device.
- The system automatically ended your session.

### What You Can Do

- Sign in again.
- Save your work regularly while creating or editing Worker Types.
- Contact your administrator if the issue occurs frequently.

---

## 10. Why is my Custom Code marked as already existing?

Each Worker Type must have a unique Custom Code.

### Possible Causes

- The Custom Code is already assigned to another Worker Type.
- The code was previously created and is still active.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a different Custom Code.
- Use your organization's naming convention to create a unique code.
- Search for the existing Worker Type to avoid creating duplicates.
