# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a new Week Off pattern?

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The Week Off Name already exists.
- The Custom Code is already in use.
- No Days Off have been selected.
- You do not have permission to create Week Off patterns.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Enter a unique Week Off Name.
- Use a unique Custom Code.
- Select at least one Day Off.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why can't I edit a Week Off pattern?

### Possible Causes

- You do not have permission to edit Week Off patterns.
- Your login session has expired.
- The Week Off pattern is currently in use by another process.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your permissions.
- Contact your administrator if necessary.

---

## 3. Why can't I delete a Week Off pattern?

### Possible Causes

- The Week Off pattern is assigned to employees or work schedules.
- The Week Off pattern is linked to an attendance policy.
- You do not have permission to delete Week Off patterns.

### What You Can Do

- Remove existing assignments before deleting.
- Assign another Week Off pattern where required.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is restricted.

---

## 4. Why is the Week Off list not loading?

### Possible Causes

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.
- A network interruption occurred.
- No Week Off patterns have been created.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Verify your internet connection.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the problem continues.

---

## 5. Why isn't the Search Week Off feature returning any results?

### Possible Causes

- The entered Week Off Name or Custom Code does not exist.
- The search keyword contains spelling mistakes.
- Applied filters are limiting the results.

### What You Can Do

- Verify the Week Off Name or Custom Code.
- Clear any applied filters.
- Try searching with a shorter keyword.

- Search the Week Off list to verify the existing code.

## 9. Why am I getting a "Duplicate Week Off Name" error?

### Possible Causes

### What You Can Do
