# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a new Leave Plan?

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The Leave Plan Name already exists.
- The Custom Code is already in use.
- No Leave Type has been selected.
- Leave days per year have not been entered.
- You do not have permission to create Leave Plans.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all mandatory fields.
- Enter a unique Leave Plan Name.
- Use a unique Custom Code.
- Select at least one Leave Type.
- Enter the leave days for each selected Leave Type.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why can't I edit a Leave Plan?

### Possible Causes

- Your account does not have edit permissions.
- The Leave Plan is currently being used by the system.
- Your login session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Sign in again.
- Verify that you have edit permissions.
- Contact your administrator if necessary.

---

## 3. Why can't I delete a Leave Plan?

### Possible Causes

- The Leave Plan is assigned to employees.
- The Leave Plan is linked to an active HR policy.
- You do not have permission to delete Leave Plans.

### What You Can Do

- Reassign employees to another Leave Plan.
- Remove policy associations.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is restricted.

---

## 4. Why can't I select a Leave Type?

### Possible Causes

- No Leave Types have been created.
- Leave Types are still loading.
- A temporary server issue occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Create Leave Types first.
- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few moments and try again.

---

## 5. Why can't I enter leave days?

### Possible Causes

- The value exceeds the allowed limit.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a valid numeric value.
- Verify your organization's leave policy.
