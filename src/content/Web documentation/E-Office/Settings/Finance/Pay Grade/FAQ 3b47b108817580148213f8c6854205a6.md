# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a Pay Grade?

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The Pay Grade Name already exists.
- The Custom Code already exists.
- A Pay Scale has not been selected.
- Salary component values are invalid.
- You do not have permission to create pay grades.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Use a unique Pay Grade Name.
- Use a unique Custom Code.
- Select a valid Pay Scale.
- Verify the salary component values.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why is the Total Amount incorrect?

### Possible Causes

- One or more salary components contain incorrect values.
- A Pay Scale has not been selected.
- The calculation has not been refreshed.

### What You Can Do

- Verify the values entered for Basic Salary, HRA, Allowances, Variables, and Bonus.
- Select the correct Pay Scale.
- Refresh the calculation or save the form again.
- Contact your administrator if the amount remains incorrect.

---

## 3. Why can't I edit a Pay Grade?

### Possible Causes

- The pay grade is already assigned to active payroll configurations.
- You do not have edit permissions.
- Your login session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Contact your administrator to obtain edit permissions.

---

## 4. Why can't I delete a Pay Grade?

### Possible Causes

- The pay grade is assigned to employees.
- The pay grade is linked to payroll processing.
- You do not have delete permission.

### What You Can Do

- Remove employee assignments before deleting.
- Ensure the pay grade is no longer used in payroll.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is restricted.

---

## 5. Why can't I find my Pay Grade?

### Possible Causes

- The Pay Grade Name or Custom Code is incorrect.
- The pay grade has been deleted.
- Search filters are hiding the result.

### What You Can Do

- Verify the Pay Grade Name or Custom Code.
- Clear the search field.
- Refresh the page and search again.

---

## 6. Why am I getting a **500 Internal Server Error**?

### Possible Causes

- The server encountered an unexpected problem.
- A backend service is temporarily unavailable.
- The application is under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue persists.

---

## 7. Why am I getting a **403 Access Denied** or **Unauthorized** message?

### Possible Causes

- Your account does not have permission to manage Pay Grades.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your assigned permissions.
- Contact your administrator if access is required.

---

## 8. Why isn't the Pay Grade list loading?

### Possible Causes

- Slow internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.
- No pay grades have been created.
- A network interruption occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Wait a few moments and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 9. Why can't I select a Pay Scale?

### Possible Causes

- No Pay Scales have been created.
- You do not have permission to view Pay Scales.
- The Pay Scale list is still loading.

### What You Can Do

- Wait for the list to load completely.
- Refresh the page.
- Verify that Pay Scales are available.
- Contact your administrator if the list remains empty.

---

## 10. Why aren't my updates reflected after editing a Pay Grade?

### Possible Causes

- The update was not saved successfully.
- Cached data is being displayed.
- A temporary synchronization delay occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the Pay Grade list.
- Verify that the update was saved successfully.
- Wait a few moments and check again.
