# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a Pay Scale?

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The Pay Scale Name already exists.
- The Custom Code already exists.
- The Frequency has not been selected.
- The salary range is invalid.
- You do not have permission to create pay scales.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Use a unique Pay Scale Name.
- Use a unique Custom Code.
- Select a valid Frequency.
- Verify the salary range values.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why am I getting an invalid salary range error?

### Possible Causes

- The Starting Range is greater than the Ending Range.
- One or both range values are missing.
- Invalid numeric values have been entered.

### What You Can Do

- Ensure the Starting Range is less than or equal to the Ending Range.
- Enter valid numeric values.
- Review the range before submitting.

---

## 3. Why can't I edit a Pay Scale?

### Possible Causes

- The pay scale is currently assigned to one or more pay grades.
- You do not have edit permission.
- Your login session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Contact your administrator to obtain edit permissions.

---

## 4. Why can't I delete a Pay Scale?

### Possible Causes

- The pay scale is assigned to pay grades.
- The pay scale is used in payroll processing.
- You do not have delete permission.

### What You Can Do

- Remove all assignments before deleting.
- Ensure the pay scale is no longer used.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is restricted.

---

## 5. Why can't I find my Pay Scale?

### Possible Causes

- The Pay Scale Name or Custom Code is incorrect.
- The pay scale has been deleted.
- Search filters are hiding the result.

### What You Can Do

- Verify the Pay Scale Name or Custom Code.
- Clear the search field.
- Refresh the page and search again.

---

## 6. Why am I getting a **500 Internal Server Error**?

### Possible Causes

- The server encountered an unexpected problem.
- A backend service is temporarily unavailable.
- The application is under maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue persists.

---

## 7. Why am I getting a **403 Access Denied** or **Unauthorized** message?

### Possible Causes

- Your account does not have permission to manage Pay Scales.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify your assigned permissions.
- Contact your administrator if access is required.

---

## 8. Why isn't the Pay Scale list loading?

### Possible Causes

- Slow internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.
- No pay scales have been created.
- A network interruption occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Wait a few moments and try again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 9. Why aren't my changes reflected after editing a Pay Scale?

### Possible Causes

- The update was not saved successfully.
- Cached information is being displayed.
- A temporary synchronization delay occurred.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the Pay Scale list.
- Verify that the update was saved successfully.
- Wait a few moments and check again.

---

## 10. Why can't I select the required Frequency?

### Possible Causes

- The page did not load completely.
- A temporary application issue occurred.
- The selected option is unavailable due to system configuration.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Try selecting the frequency again.
- Contact your administrator if the problem continues.
