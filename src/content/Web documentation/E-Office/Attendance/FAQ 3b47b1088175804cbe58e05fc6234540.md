# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I Clock In?

### Possible Causes

- You have already clocked in today.
- Your assigned shift has not started.
- Your login session has expired.
- Internet connection is unavailable.
- Attendance is disabled by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Verify your shift timing.
- Sign in again.
- Check your internet connection.
- Contact your administrator.

---

## 2. Why can't I Clock Out?

### Possible Causes

- You haven't clocked in.
- Your attendance session has already ended.
- Network interruption occurred.
- Your session expired.

### What You Can Do

- Verify that you successfully clocked in.
- Refresh the page.
- Sign in again.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 3. Why are my Total Working Hours showing as 0?

### Possible Causes

- Clock In was not completed.
- Clock Out has not been recorded.
- Attendance is still syncing.

### What You Can Do

- Verify your Clock In time.
- Wait a few moments.
- Refresh the page.

---

## 4. Why am I marked as Late Arrival?

### Possible Causes

- You clocked in after your scheduled shift start time.
- Grace period has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Check your assigned shift.
- Contact your manager if you believe this is incorrect.

---

## 5. Why can't I submit a Leave Request?

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are missing.
- Leave Type is not selected.
- End Date is earlier than Start Date.
- Insufficient leave balance.
- The selected dates overlap an existing leave request.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Select a valid Leave Type.
- Verify the selected dates.
- Check your leave balance.
- Cancel or modify existing requests if necessary.

---

## 6. Why is my Leave Request still Pending?

### Possible Causes

- Your manager has not reviewed the request.
- Approval workflow is still in progress.

### What You Can Do

- Wait for approval.
- Contact your reporting manager if the request is urgent.

---

## 7. Why can't I see my Leave Balance?

### Possible Causes

- Leave policy has not been assigned.
- Leave balances are still being calculated.
- Temporary server issue.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few minutes.
- Contact your HR administrator.

---

## 8. Why isn't my Attendance Log loading?

### Possible Causes

- Slow internet connection.
- Temporary server issue.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.

## 9. Why am I receiving a "403 Access Denied" message?

### Possible Causes

- Your role does not have permission.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted.

### What You Can Do

- Sign in again.
- Verify your permissions.
- Contact your administrator.

---

## 10. Why am I receiving a "500 Internal Server Error"?

### Possible Causes

- Temporary server issue.
- Backend service unavailable.
- System maintenance is in progress.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few minutes.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue continues.
