
# Support

![support view screen (2).png](Support/support_view_screen_(2).png)

The **Support** screen allows you to submit technical inquiries, feature requests, or account questions directly to our support team. It also provides quick access to self-help modules and Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) to help resolve common issues without waiting for a response.

---

## 1. Inquiry Form Breakdown

The main section of the Support screen contains the ticket submission form. Fill out the following fields to submit your issue:

### Ticket Classification

- **Support Type Dropdown:** Select the category that best describes your inquiry:
    - *Technical support*
    - *Billing & Payments*
    - *Account Management*
    - *Feature Request*
    - *Onboarding*
    - *Free Trial*
    - *Security & Complaints*
- **Related Module Dropdown:** Choose the specific platform module experiencing the issue:
    - *Dashboard* (Dashboard, widgets, and analytics)
    - *Employee* (Employee, roles, and hierarchy)
    - *Project* (Projects, tasks, and timeline)
    - *Timesheet* (Timesheet, approvals, and reports)
    - *Mail* (Email, templates, and configurations)
    - *Chat* (Messages, channels, and files)
    - *Billing* (Invoices, payments, and subscription)
    - *Settings* (General settings and preferences)
    - *Organization* (Organization setup and configuration)
    - *Permissions* (Roles, permissions, and access)

### Inquiry Content

- **Subject:** Enter a brief, clear summary of your issue or request (e.g., *"Cannot export monthly timesheet PDF"*).
- **Description:** Enter detailed notes explaining your problem, steps to reproduce the issue, or specific questions.

### Priority & Contact Preferences

- **Priority Radio Buttons:** Select how urgently your ticket needs to be addressed:
    - **Low:** General questions or non-critical feedback.
    - **Medium:** Standard operational questions or minor issues.
    - **High:** Significant issues impacting daily work.
    - **Critical:** System outages, security concerns, or urgent blocking issues.
- **Preferred Contact Method:** Select how you want our team to follow up with you:
    - **Email**
    - **Phone**

### Attachments & Contact Information

- **Attachment (Optional):** Drag and drop screenshots, log files, or documents into the upload box (or click the upload icon `☁️` / `↑` to browse files).
- **Contact Information Fields:**
    - **Name:** Enter your full name.
    - **Email:** Enter your active email address for ticket updates.
    - **Phone Number:** Select your country code (e.g., `+91`) and enter your direct phone number.

---

## 2. Form Buttons & Actions

- **Submit Support / Submit Ticket:** Click this blue button to finalize and send your inquiry ticket to the support team.
- **Cancel:** Click this button to clear all filled fields and reset the form.

---

## 3. Popular Support Modules & Self-Help FAQ

The right sidebar provides quick self-service resources:

### Popular Support Modules

Click on any module card to read quick reference guides and troubleshooting articles:

- **Pricing & Subscription / Billing:** Invoices, payments, and subscription plans.
- **Onboarding & Setup:** Domain verification and account setup.
- **Project & Timeline:** Task management, timelines, and project setup.
- **Employee Management:** Roles, access permissions, and hierarchy.
- **Security & Governance:** Security settings and domain policies.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Click the arrow `∨` next to any question to expand and read the answer directly on screen:

- *How to verify my domain?*
- *How do I import my employees?*
- *How do I create a project?*
- *How do I change my subscription?*
- *How can I reset my password?*
