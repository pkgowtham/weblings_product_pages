# Sign In

![enter email (2).png](Sign%20In/enter_email_(2).png)

Use your registered email address and password to access your account securely.

## Before You Begin

Make sure you have:

- A registered account.
- A verified email address.
- Your account password.

## Steps to Sign In

1. Open the **Sign In** page.
2. Enter your registered **Email Address**.
3. Enter your **Password**.
4. If you want to check your password before signing in, click the **Show Password** (👁️) icon.
5. Click **Continue**.

If your email address and password are correct, you will be signed in and redirected to your dashboard.

## Forgot Your Password?

![enter email (4).png](Sign%20In/enter_email_(4).png)

If you have forgotten your password, you can easily create a new one by verifying your email address.

On the **Sign In** page, click **Forgot Password?** to begin the password reset process.

---

# Step 1: Enter Your Email Address

The **Forgot Password** page will open.

Enter the email address that is registered with your account.

> **Note:** Make sure you enter the same email address you used when creating your account.
> 

After entering your email address, click **Send OTP**.

If you remember your password before resetting it, click **Log In** to return to the Sign In page.

---

# Step 2: Verify Your Email

After clicking **Send OTP**, a **One-Time Password (OTP)** will be sent to your registered email address.

At the same time, the website will display the **Check Your Email** page, where you will be asked to enter the OTP.

Open your email inbox and locate the email containing the verification code.

If you cannot find the email, check your **Spam**, **Junk**, or **Promotions** folder.

Enter the OTP exactly as it appears in the email.

Click **Verify**.

If the OTP is correct, you will be taken to the next step.

> **Note:** If you do not receive the OTP, you can request a new one by clicking **Resend OTP**. The **Resend OTP** option becomes available after **60 seconds**.
> 

---

# Step 3: Reset Your Password

After your email has been verified, the **Reset Your Password** page will be displayed.

Enter your new password.

Your new password must meet the following requirements:

- Be at least **8 characters** long.
- Include at least **one uppercase letter** (A–Z).
- Include at least **one lowercase letter** (a–z).
- Include at least **one number** (0–9).
- Include at least **one special character** (such as **! @ # $ % & ***).

Next, enter the same password again in the **Confirm Password** field.

This helps ensure that your new password has been entered correctly.

After completing both fields, click **Confirm**.

---

# Password Reset Successful

If your password has been updated successfully, a confirmation screen will be displayed.

You will see a message similar to:

**Your password has been updated successfully.**

**You can now sign in using your new password.**

Click **Back to Sign In** or **Log In** to return to the Sign In page.

Enter your email address and your new password to access your account.

```
    ↓

Password Reset Successful
    ↓

```

## Don't Have an Account?

If you're a new user:

1. Click **Sign Up**.
2. Complete the registration process.
3. Verify your email address.
4. Return to the Sign In page and log in with your new account.

## Common Sign-In Issues

### Incorrect Email or Password

- Make sure your email address is entered correctly.
- Check that your password is correct.
- Ensure that **Caps Lock** is turned off.
- Try entering your password again.

### Email Not Verified

If your email address has not been verified, complete the email verification process before attempting to sign in.
