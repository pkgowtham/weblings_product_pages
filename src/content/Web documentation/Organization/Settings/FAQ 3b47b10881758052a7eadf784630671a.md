# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I save my settings?

Your changes may not be saved if required information is missing or validation fails.

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are incomplete.
- Invalid employee code settings.
- Your session has expired.
- Temporary server issue.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Verify the employee code settings.
- Sign in again if your session has expired.
- Try saving again after a few moments.

---

## 2. Why didn't my theme change after clicking **Save Changes**?

The new theme is applied only after the changes are successfully saved.

### Possible Causes

- Changes were not saved successfully.
- Temporary browser issue.
- Network interruption.

### What You Can Do

- Click **Save Changes** again.
- Refresh the page.
- Sign out and sign back in if necessary.

---

## 3. Why is the preview different from the actual appearance?

The preview is only a visual representation before changes are applied.

### Possible Causes

- Changes have not been saved.
- Browser cache is displaying the previous theme.

### What You Can Do

- Save your changes.
- Refresh the page.
- Clear your browser cache if the issue continues.

---

## 4. Why can't I change the accent color?

Accent colors can only be changed if customization is enabled.

### Possible Causes

- Theme settings failed to load.
- Temporary application issue.
- Network interruption.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait for the settings to finish loading.
- Try again after reconnecting to the internet.

---

## 5. Why is the **Organization Settings** tab unavailable?

Only users with the required permissions can modify organization settings.

### Possible Causes

- Your role does not have administrative privileges.
- Organization settings are restricted.
- Your session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Sign in again.
- Contact your administrator for access.
- Verify your assigned role and permissions.

---

## 6. Why can't I edit the employee code settings?

Employee code settings may be restricted to authorized users.

### Possible Causes

- Insufficient permissions.
- Organization settings are locked.
- Employee code generation is managed by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Contact your administrator.
- Verify that your account has permission to manage organization settings.

---

## 7. Why is the employee code example not updating?

The example updates automatically based on the values entered.

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are incomplete.
- Invalid values were entered.
- Temporary page issue.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all employee code fields.
- Verify the entered values.
- Refresh the page if necessary.

---

## 8. Why is my employee code format invalid?

Employee code generation follows the configured numbering rules.

### Possible Causes

- Prefix contains unsupported characters.
- Start From contains invalid values.
- Step Increment is invalid.

### What You Can Do

- Use a valid prefix.
- Enter numeric values for **Start From** and **Step Increment**.
- Review the generated example before saving.

---

## 9. Why can't I use the same employee code prefix?

Employee code prefixes should be configured according to your organization's naming standards.

### Possible Causes

- The prefix does not meet organization requirements.
- Invalid characters have been entered.

### What You Can Do

- Use letters or approved characters only.
- Follow your organization's employee code format.

---

## 10. What happens if I click **Discard**?

Clicking **Discard** removes all unsaved changes.

### What You Can Do

- Click **Discard** only if you do not want to keep your changes.
- Click **Save Changes** before leaving the page if you want to keep your updates.

---

## 11. Why am I getting a **403 Access Denied** or **Unauthorized** message?

Your account does not have permission to access or modify the Settings module.

### Possible Causes

- Your assigned role does not include Settings permissions.
- Your session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify that you are using the correct account.
- Contact your administrator to request access.

---

## 12. Why am I seeing a **404 Page Not Found** error?

The requested Settings page could not be found.

### Possible Causes

- The page has been moved or removed.
- The URL is incorrect.
- Temporary navigation issue.

### What You Can Do

- Return to the dashboard.
- Open the Settings page again.
- Refresh the page.

---

## 13. Why am I seeing a **500 Internal Server Error**?

A **500 Internal Server Error** indicates that the server encountered an unexpected problem.

### Possible Causes

- Temporary server outage.
- Database connection issue.
- Internal application error.
- Scheduled maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Try again later.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 14. Why am I seeing a **Network Error**?

A Network Error occurs when the application cannot communicate with the server.

### Possible Causes

- Internet connection lost.
- VPN or firewall restrictions.
- Temporary server outage.

### What You Can Do

- Verify your internet connection.
- Refresh the page.
- Disable VPN if applicable.
- Try again later.

---

## 15. Why were my changes lost?

Unsaved changes are discarded when you leave the page or your session expires.

### Possible Causes

- You clicked **Discard**.
- Browser was refreshed before saving.
- Session expired.
- Browser was closed.

### What You Can Do

- Click **Save Changes** before leaving the page.
- Avoid refreshing the browser while editing settings.
- Sign in again if your session expires.

---

## 16. Why can't I switch between Light Mode and Dark Mode?

The application may be unable to switch themes due to a temporary issue.

### Possible Causes

- Theme settings failed to load.
- Browser compatibility issue.
- Temporary application error.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Try another supported browser.
- Save the theme again after making your selection.

---

## 17. Why do I need to configure employee code settings?

Employee code settings ensure that every employee receives a unique and consistent employee ID.

### Benefits

- Prevents duplicate employee codes.
- Maintains a standardized employee numbering system.
- Simplifies employee identification and reporting.

---

## 18. Can I change employee code settings after employees have been created?

Yes, but changing these settings may affect how future employee codes are generated.

### Recommendation

- Review your numbering format carefully before saving.
- Coordinate changes with your HR or system administrator to maintain consistency.
