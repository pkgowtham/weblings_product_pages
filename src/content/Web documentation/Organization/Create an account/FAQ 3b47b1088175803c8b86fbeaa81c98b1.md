# FAQ

## 1. Why didn't I receive the Email OTP?

If you do not receive the OTP after creating your account or enabling Two-Step Verification, there may be several reasons.

### Possible Causes

- The email address was entered incorrectly.
- The email is in your Spam, Junk, or Promotions folder.
- Your email provider is experiencing a delay.
- Your mailbox is full.

### What You Can Do

- Wait for a few minutes.
- Check your Spam, Junk, or Promotions folders.
- Confirm that you entered the correct email address.
- Click **Resend OTP** after the 60-second countdown.
- Contact support if you still do not receive the OTP.

---

## 2. Why didn't I receive the Mobile OTP?

If you do not receive the SMS verification code, one of the following may be the cause.

### Possible Causes

- The mobile number was entered incorrectly.
- Poor mobile network coverage.
- SMS services are temporarily delayed.
- The selected country code is incorrect.

### What You Can Do

- Verify your country code and mobile number.
- Ensure your phone has network coverage.
- Wait a few minutes.
- Click **Resend OTP** after 60 seconds.
- Contact your mobile service provider if SMS messages are not being received.

---

## 3. Why does the OTP show as Invalid?

An invalid OTP means the code entered does not match the latest OTP generated by the system.

### Possible Causes

- The OTP was entered incorrectly.
- A new OTP was requested, making the previous OTP invalid.
- The OTP belongs to another email address or mobile number.

### What You Can Do

- Enter the OTP exactly as received.
- Use only the most recent OTP.
- Request a new OTP if necessary.

---

## 4. Why does the OTP expire?

For security reasons, OTPs remain valid only for a limited time.

### Possible Causes

- Too much time passed before entering the OTP.
- A newer OTP was generated.

### What You Can Do

- Click **Resend OTP** after the countdown ends.
- Enter the newly received OTP.

---

## 5. Why can't I create my account?

If clicking **Confirm** does not create your account, some information may be missing or invalid.

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are empty.
- Password does not meet security requirements.
- Password and Confirm Password do not match.
- The email address is already registered.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Create a password that meets all password requirements.
- Ensure both password fields match.
- Use a different email address if the current one is already registered.

---

## 6. Why am I seeing "Email Already Exists"?

This means an account has already been created using the same email address.

### Possible Causes

- You have already registered.
- Someone else has already registered using that email address.

### What You Can Do

- Click **Sign In** if you already have an account.
- Use **Forgot Password** if you cannot remember your password.
- Use another email address if necessary.

---

## 7. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error?

A 500 Internal Server Error indicates that the server encountered an unexpected problem.

### Possible Causes

- Temporary server issue.
- System maintenance.
- Backend service interruption.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few minutes.
- Sign in again.
- Contact your system administrator if the problem continues.

---

## 8. Why am I getting a Network Error?

A Network Error means the application cannot communicate with the server.

### Possible Causes

- Poor internet connection.
- Firewall, VPN, or proxy blocking the connection.
- Temporary server outage.

### What You Can Do

- Check your internet connection.
- Refresh the page.
- Disable your VPN if applicable.
- Try again later.

---

## 9. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied or Unauthorized message?

This message indicates that you do not have permission to access the requested page.

### Possible Causes

- Your login session has expired.
- You attempted to access a restricted page.
- Your account permissions are limited.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify that you are using the correct account.
- Contact your administrator if you believe you should have access.

---

## 10. Why was I automatically logged out?

For security reasons, your session may expire after a period of inactivity.

### Possible Causes

- Session timeout.
- You signed in from another device.
- Your administrator ended your session.

### What You Can Do

- Sign in again.
- Save your work regularly.
- Contact your administrator if this happens frequently.

---

## 11. Can I skip Email or Mobile Verification?

No.

Both email verification and mobile number verification are required to protect your account and ensure secure access to the application.

---

## 12. Can I enable Two-Step Verification later?

Yes.

If you choose **Continue without Two-Step Verification**, you can enable it later from your **Profile** or **Security Settings** whenever you want additional account security.
