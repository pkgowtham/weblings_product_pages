# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a Business Unit?

If clicking **Create Business Unit** does not create a new business unit, the required information may be incomplete or invalid.

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The Business Unit name already exists.
- The Subdomain is already in use.
- Invalid address information has been entered.
- You do not have permission to create business units.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Enter a unique Business Unit name.
- Choose a unique Subdomain.
- Verify the address details.
- Contact your administrator if you do not have the required permissions.

---

## 2. Why can't I create a Business Location?

A Business Location can only be created after selecting a valid Business Unit.

### Possible Causes

- A Business Unit has not been created.
- Required location information is missing.
- Invalid address information has been entered.
- The selected Business Unit is unavailable.

### What You Can Do

- Create a Business Unit first.
- Complete all required location fields.
- Verify the selected Business Unit.
- Refresh the page and try again.

---

## 3. Why are the login credentials not displayed?

Login credentials are generated automatically after a Business Location is successfully created.

### Possible Causes

- The Business Location was not created successfully.
- Credential generation is still in progress.
- A temporary server delay occurred.
- Network connectivity issues.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Click **Show Email and Password** again.
- Verify that the Business Location was created successfully.
- Wait a few moments and try again.

---

## 4. Why can't I update the login credentials?

If the updated email address or password is not being saved, validation may have failed.

### Possible Causes

- The email address is invalid.
- The email address is already in use.
- The password does not meet the required password policy.
- Your session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Enter a valid email address.
- Use a unique email address.
- Create a password that meets all password requirements.
- Refresh the page and sign in again if necessary.

---

## 5. Why can't I sign in to eOffice using the generated credentials?

If the generated credentials do not work, the account may not yet be ready for use.

### Possible Causes

- Incorrect email address or password.
- The credentials were recently updated.
- Account activation is still in progress.
- Typing or capitalization errors.

### What You Can Do

- Verify the email address carefully.
- Re-enter the password exactly as shown.
- Wait a few minutes after updating the credentials.
- Reset the password if necessary.

---

## 6. Why is the Subdomain unavailable?

Each Business Unit must have a unique Subdomain.

### Possible Causes

- Another Business Unit is already using the Subdomain.
- The Subdomain contains unsupported characters.
- The Subdomain does not follow the required naming rules.

### What You Can Do

- Choose a different Subdomain.
- Use only supported characters.
- Try adding your company or location name to make it unique.

---

## 7. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied message?

A 403 error indicates that your account does not have permission to perform the requested action.

### Possible Causes

- Your assigned role does not include Organization Setup permissions.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify that you are using the correct administrator account.
- Contact your system administrator to request the required permissions.

---

## 8. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error during Organization Setup?

A 500 Internal Server Error indicates that the server encountered an unexpected problem while processing your request.

### Possible Causes

- Temporary server issue.
- Backend service unavailable.
- Database connectivity problem.
- Scheduled maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 9. Why is the Business Unit or Location list not loading?

If the list remains empty or continues loading, the data may not have been retrieved successfully.

### Possible Causes

- Slow internet connection.
- Temporary network interruption.
- Server response delay.
- No Business Units or Locations have been created.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Verify your internet connection.
- Wait a few moments for the data to load.
- Confirm that Business Units and Locations have already been created.

---

## 10. Why can't I see the Edit option for login credentials?

The Edit option is available only to users with the required administrative permissions.

### Possible Causes

- Your role does not include credential management permissions.
- The Business Location has not been created.
- Your session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Verify that you are signed in with an administrator account.
- Refresh the page and sign in again.
- Contact your system administrator if you require additional permissions.
