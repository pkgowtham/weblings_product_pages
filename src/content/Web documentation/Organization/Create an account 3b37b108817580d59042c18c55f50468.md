

# Create an Account
![Verify OTP (1).png](Create%20an%20account/Verify_OTP_(1).png)

Creating an account is the first step to accessing the platform. The registration process only takes a few minutes.

## Step 1: Open the Sign Up Page

Navigate to the **Sign Up** page to begin creating your account.

---

## Step 2: Enter Your Information

Complete all the required fields to create your account.

### Full Name

Enter your full name as you would like it to appear in your account.

**Example:**

```
John Smith
```

Using your real name makes it easier for your organization and team members to identify you.

---

### Email Address

Enter a valid email address that you have access to.

This email address will be used to:

- Verify your account.
- Receive important notifications.
- Reset your password if you forget it.
- Receive verification codes (OTP) when required.

**Example:**

```
john.smith@example.com
```

> **Note:** Make sure you enter your email address correctly, as it will be used throughout the application.
> 

---

### Password

Create a strong password to help keep your account secure.

Your password must meet the following requirements:

- Be at least **8 characters** long.
- Include at least **one uppercase letter** (A–Z).
- Include at least **one lowercase letter** (a–z).
- Include at least **one number** (0–9).
- Include at least **one special character**, such as **! @ # $ % & \**.

**Example of a strong password:**

```
1h@i0=dx_@uyu~1jDC6[rgx
```

Choose a password that is easy for you to remember but difficult for others to guess.

> **Tip:** Avoid using personal information such as your name, date of birth, or phone number in your password.
> 

---

### Confirm Password

Re-enter the same password you created in the **Password** field.

This helps ensure that your password has been entered correctly.

If the passwords do not match, you will be asked to enter them again before continuing.

---

## Step 3: Create Your Account

After reviewing your information, click **Confirm** to create your account.

If all the information is valid, your account will be created successfully, and you will be guided to the next step to verify your email address.

---

# Verify Your Email

![Verify OTP (2).png](Create%20an%20account/Verify_OTP_(2).png)

After you have successfully created your account, the next step is to verify your email address.

To verify your account, a **One-Time Password (OTP)** will be sent to the email address you used during registration.

At the same time, the website will display the **Verify Email** page, where you will be asked to enter the OTP.

## How to Verify Your Email

### Step 1

Open the email inbox of the email address you used to create your account.

### Step 2

Look for the email containing your **One-Time Password (OTP)**.

If you cannot find the email, check your **Spam**, **Junk**, or **Promotions** folder.

### Step 3

Return to the **Verify Email** **address** page on the website.

### Step 4

Enter the OTP exactly as it appears in the email.

### Step 5

Click **Verify OTP**.

If the OTP is correct, your email address will be verified successfully, and you can continue to the next step.

---

## Didn't Receive the OTP?

If you haven't received the OTP:

- Wait a few moments, as email delivery may take a short time.
- Check your **Spam**, **Junk**, or **Promotions** folder.
- Make sure you entered the correct email address during registration.
- You can request a new OTP by clicking **Resend OTP**.

> **Note:** The **Resend OTP** option becomes available after **60 seconds**. If your OTP expires or you didn't receive it, simply click **Resend OTP** to receive a new verification code.
> 

---

# Verify Your Mobile Number

![2FA-enter mbile number (2).png](Create%20an%20account/2FA-enter_mbile_number_(2).png)

After your email address has been verified, the next step is to verify your mobile number.

The website will display the **Enter Mobile Number** page.

Your mobile number is used only for account authentication and security. Please enter a valid mobile number that can receive SMS messages.

---

## Step 1: Enter Your Mobile Number

Select your country code from the list.

Enter your active mobile number in the provided field.

> **Note:** Make sure the mobile number is correct, as the verification code (OTP) will be sent to this number.
> 

After entering your mobile number, click **Send OTP**.

---

## Step 2: Receive the OTP

Once you click **Send OTP**, a **One-Time Password (OTP)** will be sent to your mobile number via SMS.

At the same time, the website will automatically display the **Verify OTP** page.

---

## Step 3: Verify the OTP

Open the SMS message on your mobile phone and note the OTP you received.

Return to the **Verify OTP** page on the website.

Enter the OTP exactly as it appears in the SMS.

Click **Verify OTP**.

If the OTP is correct, your mobile number will be verified successfully, and you can continue to the next step.

---

## Didn't Receive the OTP?

If you haven't received the OTP:

- Wait a few moments, as SMS delivery may take a short time.
- Make sure you entered the correct mobile number.
- Ensure your mobile phone has network coverage and can receive SMS messages.

If you still haven't received the OTP, click **Resend OTP** to request a new verification code.

> **Note:** The **Resend OTP** option becomes available after **60 seconds**. If your OTP expires or you do not receive it, you can request a new OTP and try again.
> 

---

## Verification Successful

Once your OTP is verified, your mobile number will be linked to your account.

Your account is now secured with a verified mobile number, and you can continue to the next step of the setup process.

---

# Enable Two-Step Verification

![2FA-enter mbile number (3).png](Create%20an%20account/2FA-enter_mbile_number_(3).png)

After you have successfully verified your email address and mobile number, the next step is to choose whether you want to enable **Two-Step Verification** for your account.

Two-Step Verification adds an extra layer of security to your account. When enabled, you will receive a verification code in your email each time you sign in. This helps protect your account from unauthorized access.

On this screen, you will see two options:

- **Enable Two-Step Verification**
- **Continue without Two-Step Verification**

---

# Option 1: Enable Two-Step Verification

If you choose **Enable Two-Step Verification**, the system will send a **One-Time Password (OTP)** to your registered email address.

At the same time, the website will display the **Verify OTP** page.

## Step 1

Open your email inbox and find the email containing the OTP.

If you cannot find the email, check your **Spam**, **Junk**, or **Promotions** folder.

---

## Step 2

Return to the **Verify OTP** page on the website.

Enter the OTP exactly as it appears in the email.

---

## Step 3

Click **Verify OTP**.

If the OTP is correct, Two-Step Verification will be enabled for your account.

A confirmation screen will appear with the message **"Two-Step Verification Enabled"**.

Click **Finish** to continue.

From now on, every time you sign in, a verification code will be sent to your registered email address. You must enter this code to complete the sign-in process.

---

## Didn't Receive the OTP?

If you haven't received the verification email:

- Wait a few moments, as email delivery may take a short time.
- Check your **Spam**, **Junk**, or **Promotions** folder.
- Make sure you have access to your registered email address.
- Click **Resend OTP** to request a new verification code.

> **Note:** The **Resend OTP** option becomes available after **60 seconds**. If your OTP expires or you do not receive it, you can request a new one.
> 

---

# Option 2: Continue without Two-Step Verification

If you choose **Continue without Two-Step Verification**, you can continue using the application without enabling this additional security feature.

You will be able to sign in using only your email address and password.

> **Note:** You can enable Two-Step Verification later from your **Profile** or **Security Settings** if you decide to add extra protection to your account.
> 

---

# Why Enable Two-Step Verification?

Enabling Two-Step Verification helps keep your account secure by requiring an additional verification code whenever you sign in.

Benefits include:

- Adds an extra layer of protection to your account.
- Helps prevent unauthorized access.
- Protects your personal and organization information.
- Increases the security of your account even if your password is compromised.
