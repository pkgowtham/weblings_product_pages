# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I continue to the next step during Organization Setup?

If the **Next** button is disabled or the setup does not continue, some required information may be missing or invalid.

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- An invalid email, identification number, or postal code has been entered.
- Required dropdown fields have not been selected.
- The entered information does not meet validation requirements.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Verify that all information is entered correctly.
- Check for validation messages displayed below the fields.
- Try submitting the information again.

---

## 2. Why is my preferred domain unavailable?

A domain may already be registered or reserved by another organization.

### Possible Causes

- The domain name is already in use.
- The selected domain has already been purchased.
- The requested domain violates naming rules.

### What You Can Do

- Try another domain name.
- Add numbers or your company initials to create a unique domain.
- Use the free Weblings domain if available.
- Search for another available domain.

---

## 3. Why did my domain verification fail?

Domain verification confirms that you own the domain before it can be connected to your organization.

### Possible Causes

- The TXT verification record has not been added.
- The TXT record was entered incorrectly.
- DNS changes have not finished propagating.
- The verification record has been removed.

### What You Can Do

- Verify that the TXT record exactly matches the provided value.
- Wait for DNS propagation, which may take some time.
- Run the verification again.
- Contact your domain provider if the record cannot be added.

---

## 4. Why did my payment fail during checkout?

A payment failure prevents your subscription or domain purchase from being completed.

### Possible Causes

- Insufficient account balance.
- Card or payment method was declined.
- Payment gateway temporarily unavailable.
- Network interruption during payment.

### What You Can Do

- Verify your payment information.
- Try another payment method.
- Check your internet connection.
- Retry the payment after a few minutes.
- Contact your payment provider if the issue continues.

---

## 5. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error during Organization Setup?

A 500 error indicates that the server encountered an unexpected problem while processing your request.

### Possible Causes

- Temporary server issue.
- Backend services are unavailable.
- Database connectivity problem.
- Scheduled maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few minutes and try again.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Contact your system administrator if the error continues.

---

## 6. Why can't I access the Accounts or Billing module?

The Accounts and Billing modules may not be available for every user.

### Possible Causes

- Your role does not include the required permissions.
- Organization setup has not been completed.
- Your subscription has expired.
- Your login session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Complete the Organization Setup process.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify that your subscription is active.
- Contact your administrator to request access.

---

## 7. Why are my organization details not updating?

Changes to company, location, or finance information may not be saved if validation fails.

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are incomplete.
- Invalid data has been entered.
- Your session expired while editing.
- You do not have permission to edit organization information.

### What You Can Do

- Review all required fields.
- Correct any validation errors.
- Save the changes again.
- Refresh the page and retry.
- Contact your administrator if you cannot edit the information.

---

## 8. Why can't I view my payment history or transactions?

Payment information is available only after successful payments have been processed.

### Possible Causes

- No completed payments exist.
- Payment processing is still pending.
- Network connection problem.
- Insufficient permissions.

### What You Can Do

- Wait a few minutes if payment was recently completed.
- Refresh the page.
- Verify your internet connection.
- Contact your administrator if payment records are still missing.

---

## 9. Why is my subscription plan not activated after payment?

In some cases, subscription activation may take a short time after payment is completed.

### Possible Causes

- Payment confirmation is still being processed.
- Payment was unsuccessful.
- Subscription activation is pending.
- Temporary server delay.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Check the Payment History section.
- Verify that the payment status is **Successful**.
- Contact support if the subscription is not activated after a reasonable period.

---

## 10. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied or Unauthorized message?

A 403 error means your account is not authorized to access the requested page.

### Possible Causes

- Your assigned role does not have permission.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your organization.
- Your account has been disabled.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify that you are using the correct account.
- Contact your administrator to request the required permissions.
- Ensure your account is active.
