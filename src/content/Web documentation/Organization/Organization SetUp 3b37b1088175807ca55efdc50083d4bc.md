# Organization SetUp

![Organization SetUp](Organization%20SetUp/organization-organization-setup.png)

After creating your organization, the next step is to set up your business units and their locations. This helps you organize different departments, branches, or offices under your organization.

Each business location will automatically receive its own login credentials, which can be used to access eOffice.

---

# Step 1: Create a Business Unit

Click **Create Business Unit** to add your first business unit.

A business unit can represent a department, division, branch, or line of business within your organization.

## Enter the following details:

- Business Unit Name
- Description
- Address Line 1
- Address Line 2 (Optional)
- Country
- State
- City
- ZIP Code
- Subdomain

After filling in the required information, click **Create Business Unit**.

Your business unit will be added to the list.

---

# Business Unit List

![Business Unit List](Organization%20SetUp/organization-organization-setup_business-unit-list.png)

After a business unit is created, it will appear on the screen with its basic information.

For each business unit, you can:

- View its details
- Create one or more business locations

Click **Create Location** to continue.

---

# Step 2: Create a Business Location

![Step 2: Create a Business Location](Organization%20SetUp/organization-organization-setup_step-2-create-a-business-location.png)

A business location represents a physical office, branch, warehouse, factory, or any workplace that belongs to the selected business unit.

Enter the following details:

- Location Name
- Description
- Address Line 1
- Address Line 2 (Optional)
- Country
- State
- City
- ZIP Code

After entering the required information, click **Create Location**.

You can create multiple locations under the same business unit whenever needed.

---

# View Login Credentials

![View Login Credentials](Organization%20SetUp/organization-organization-setup_view-login-credentials.png)

Once a business location is created, the system automatically creates login credentials for that location.

Click **Show Email and Password** to view the generated credentials.

The following information will be displayed:

- Email Address
- Password

These credentials can be used to log in to eOffice.

---

# Edit Login Credentials

![Edit Login Credentials](Organization%20SetUp/organization-organization-setup_edit-login-credentials.png)

If you need to update the login credentials for a business location, click the **Edit** icon.

You can update the following information:

- Email Address
- Password

---

## Update Email Address

Enter a valid email address that will be used to sign in to eOffice.

Make sure the email address:

- Is entered correctly.
- Is active and accessible.
- Can receive important notifications, if required.

**Example:**

```
chennai.office@company.com
```

---

## Update Password

Create a strong password to keep the account secure.

Your password must meet the following requirements:

- Be at least **8 characters** long.
- Include at least **one uppercase letter** (A–Z).
- Include at least **one lowercase letter** (a–z).
- Include at least **one number** (0–9).
- Include at least **one special character**, such as **! @ # $ % & \**.

**Example of a strong password:**

```
Office@123
```

> **Tip:** Avoid using personal information, such as your name or date of birth, in your password.
> 

---

## Save Your Changes

After updating the email address or password, click **Confirm**.

If all the information is valid, the updated login credentials will be saved successfully.

You can now use the new email address and password to sign in to eOffice.

---

# Sign in to eOffice

Use the email address and password assigned to the business location to sign in to eOffice.

Each business location has its own login credentials.

---

# Setup Complete

Congratulations!

You have successfully completed the organization setup.

Your organization is now ready to use.

You can continue by:

- Creating additional business units
- Adding more business locations
- Sharing login credentials with the appropriate users
- Signing in to eOffice

---
