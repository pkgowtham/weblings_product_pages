# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I save changes to my profile?

If your profile information is not updating after clicking **Save Changes**, some required information may be missing or invalid.

### Possible Causes

- One or more required fields are empty.
- The email address or phone number is in an invalid format.
- Your session has expired.
- You do not have permission to update your profile.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Verify that your email address and contact number are entered correctly.
- Refresh the page and sign in again if necessary.
- Try saving the changes again.

---

## 2. Why am I not receiving the OTP for email verification?

An OTP is sent to verify ownership of your secondary email address.

### Possible Causes

- The email address was entered incorrectly.
- The email was delivered to the Spam, Junk, or Promotions folder.
- Email delivery is temporarily delayed.
- The email server is unavailable.

### What You Can Do

- Verify that the email address is correct.
- Check your Spam, Junk, or Promotions folder.
- Wait a few minutes for the email to arrive.
- Click **Resend OTP** after the 60-second countdown has completed.

---

## 3. Why am I not receiving the OTP on my mobile number?

An OTP is sent via SMS to verify your secondary contact number or confirm password changes.

### Possible Causes

- The mobile number was entered incorrectly.
- Your device has poor network coverage.
- SMS delivery is delayed by your mobile carrier.
- The mobile number cannot receive SMS messages.

### What You Can Do

- Verify that your mobile number is correct.
- Ensure your phone has network coverage.
- Wait a few minutes before requesting another OTP.
- Click **Resend OTP** after 60 seconds.

---

## 4. Why can't I change my password?

Your password will only be updated after all validation requirements and OTP verification are completed successfully.

### Possible Causes

- The current password is incorrect.
- The new password does not meet the required password policy.
- The confirmation password does not match.
- OTP verification failed.

### What You Can Do

- Verify your current password.
- Create a password that meets all password requirements.
- Ensure the new password and confirmation password match.
- Complete the OTP verification process.

---

## 5. Why does the OTP show as invalid or expired?

For security reasons, OTPs are valid only for a limited time.

### Possible Causes

- The OTP has expired.
- An incorrect OTP was entered.
- A newer OTP was generated after clicking **Resend OTP**.

### What You Can Do

- Enter the most recent OTP you received.
- Request a new OTP if the current one has expired.
- Complete verification before the OTP expires.

---

## 6. Why can't I enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)?

MFA can only be enabled after the authentication email address has been successfully verified.

### Possible Causes

- The entered email address is invalid.
- OTP verification was not completed.
- The verification code has expired.
- Temporary server or network issue.

### What You Can Do

- Verify that the authentication email is correct.
- Complete OTP verification.
- Request a new OTP if required.
- Refresh the page and try again.

---

## 7. Why can't I disable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)?

Disabling MFA requires identity verification to protect your account.

### Possible Causes

- OTP verification was unsuccessful.
- The connected authentication email is inaccessible.
- The OTP has expired.
- Email delivery is delayed.

### What You Can Do

- Access the connected authentication email account.
- Enter the latest OTP received.
- Request a new OTP after 60 seconds if necessary.
- Contact your administrator if you no longer have access to the authentication email.

---

## 8. Why can't I upload or change my profile picture?

Profile pictures must meet the application's supported requirements.

### Possible Causes

- Unsupported image format.
- Image size exceeds the allowed limit.
- Network interruption during upload.
- Temporary server issue.

### What You Can Do

- Upload a supported image format (such as JPG or PNG).
- Reduce the image size if necessary.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Ensure you have a stable internet connection.

---

## 9. Why am I seeing a 403 Access Denied message on the Profile page?

A 403 error indicates that your account does not have permission to perform the requested action.

### Possible Causes

- Your session has expired.
- Your account permissions have changed.
- Your account has been disabled or restricted.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify that you are using the correct account.
- Contact your system administrator if access has been restricted.

---

## 10. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error while updating my profile?

A 500 Internal Server Error indicates that the server encountered an unexpected problem while processing your request.

### Possible Causes

- Temporary server issue.
- Backend service unavailable.
- Database connectivity problem.
- Scheduled maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page and try again.
- Wait a few minutes before retrying.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue continues.
