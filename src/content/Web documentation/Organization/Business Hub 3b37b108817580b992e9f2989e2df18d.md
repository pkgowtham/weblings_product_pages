# Business Hub

![Business Units (6).png](Business%20Hub/Business_Units_(6).png)

The **Business Hub** helps you manage your business units, locations, clients, projects, teams, and employees from one place.

When you open the **Business Hub**, all your business units are displayed as cards.

Click on any business unit to open it. By default, the first business location of the selected business unit will be displayed.

You can switch between different **Business Units** and **Business Locations** at any time to view and manage their information.

---

# Dashboard

![Dashboard (19).png](Business%20Hub/Dashboard_(19).png)

The Dashboard provides a quick overview of your business location.

It displays important information such as:

- Total Projects
- Total Clients
- Total Employees
- Pending Timesheets
- Total Teams

It also includes visual reports and recent updates to help you monitor your business.

### Dashboard Widgets

- Project Overview (Pie Chart)
- Timesheet Overview
- Employee Overview
- Top Clients
- Team Performance
- Project Health
- Recent Activities
- Upcoming Deadlines
- Quick Actions

The dashboard gives you a quick summary of your business without opening each module individually.

---

# Clients

![Client details (7).png](Business%20Hub/Client_details_(7).png)

The **Clients** section contains all your client information.

Here you can:

- View client details
- Add a new client
- Delete an existing client

## Add a Client

Click **Add Client** and enter the following information:

### Basic Information

- Client Name
- Client Code
- Billing Currency
- Billing Rate
- Client Manager
- Description

### Contact Information

- Address
- Country
- GST Number

After entering the required information, click **Add Client**.

The client will be added to your client list.

---

# Client Details

Click on a client to view more information.

The client details page includes:

- Dashboard
- Estimated Revenue
- Projects
- Client Profile
- Activity Log

This helps you track the client's projects, revenue, and recent activities.

---

# Projects

![Project (6).png](Business%20Hub/Project_(6).png)

The **Projects** section displays all projects as cards.

Move your mouse over a project card to see additional options.

You can:

- View Project
- Edit Project
- Delete Project

You can also create a new project using the **Add Project** button.

## Create a Project

Enter the following details:

- Project Name
- Client
- Project Duration
- Billing Type
- Project Manager

Click **Create Project** to save the project.

---

# Project Details

Selecting **View Project** opens the complete project information.

Here you can view project details, assigned team members, progress, and other related information.

---

# Teams

![Team list (3).png](Business%20Hub/Team_list_(3).png)

The **Teams** section helps you organize employees into project teams.

You can:

- View all teams
- Create a new team
- Add members to a team

## Create a Team

Enter the following details:

- Select Project
- Team Name
- Description
- Team Lead

You have two options:

### Create Team, Add Members Later

Creates the team immediately. Team members can be added later.

### Add Team Members

Select this option to choose team members before creating the team.

A list of employees in your organization will be displayed.

Select the required members and click **Create Team**.

The selected employees will become part of the new team.

---

# Employee Details

![Create team (2).png](Business%20Hub/Create_team_(2).png)

The **Employee Details** section displays all employees in the selected business location.

Each employee card shows:

- Employee Name
- Email or Domain Name
- Role
- Assigned Projects

This screen helps you quickly identify which projects each employee is working on.

        ↓

# Quick Summary

The **Business Hub** allows you to:

- Switch between business units and locations
- Monitor business performance through the dashboard
- Manage client information
- Create and manage projects
- Organize teams
- Assign employees to teams
- View employee information and project assignments

Everything you need to manage your business operations is available in one place within the Business Hub.
