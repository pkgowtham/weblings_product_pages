# FAQ

## 1. Why is the Business Hub not loading?

The Business Hub may fail to load due to a temporary server or network issue.

### Possible Causes

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- The server is temporarily unavailable.
- Your session has expired.
- The selected Business Unit is unavailable.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Select another Business Unit if available.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why can't I see any Business Units?

If no Business Units are displayed, your account may not have access to any units.

### Possible Causes

- No Business Units have been created.
- You are not assigned to any Business Unit.
- Your account does not have permission to access Business Hub.

### What You Can Do

- Verify that Business Units have been created.
- Ask your administrator to assign you to a Business Unit.
- Refresh the page after access has been granted.

---

## 3. Why can't I switch Business Locations?

Business Locations are available only for the selected Business Unit.

### Possible Causes

- The selected Business Unit has no locations.
- You do not have permission to access the selected location.
- Location information is still loading.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Wait a few moments and try again.
- Verify that the Business Unit contains at least one location.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 4. Why is the Dashboard showing no data?

Dashboard widgets display information only when data is available for the selected Business Location.

### Possible Causes

- No clients, projects, teams, or employees have been created.
- The selected Business Location has no records.
- Dashboard data is still loading.

### What You Can Do

- Confirm that data has been created for the selected location.
- Switch to another Business Location.
- Refresh the dashboard.

---

## 5. Why can't I create a new Client?

Client creation requires all mandatory information to be completed correctly.

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are empty.
- A client with the same name or client code already exists.
- Invalid billing information.
- You do not have permission to create clients.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Use a unique Client Name and Client Code.
- Verify the billing information.
- Contact your administrator if you require additional permissions.

---

## 6. Why can't I edit or delete a Client?

Some client records may be restricted from modification.

### Possible Causes

- The client is linked to active projects.
- You do not have edit or delete permission.
- The client is currently being updated by another user.

### What You Can Do

- Ensure the client is not linked to active projects.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Contact your administrator for the required permissions.

---

## 7. Why can't I create a new Project?

Projects require valid information before they can be created.

### Possible Causes

- No client has been selected.
- Required fields are incomplete.
- A project with the same name already exists.
- You do not have permission to create projects.

### What You Can Do

- Select a valid client.
- Complete all required fields.
- Use a unique project name.
- Contact your administrator if access is restricted.

---

## 8. Why can't I delete a Project?

Projects that are actively being used may not be removable.

### Possible Causes

- The project has assigned team members.
- Active tasks exist within the project.
- The project is currently in progress.
- You do not have permission to delete projects.

### What You Can Do

- Remove project dependencies if permitted.
- Complete or archive the project.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is required.

---

## 9. Why is the Team Lead list empty?

Only eligible employees can be selected as Team Leads.

### Possible Causes

- No employees exist in the selected Business Location.
- Employees are inactive.
- Employee information is still loading.

### What You Can Do

- Verify that employee records have been created.
- Refresh the page.
- Wait until the employee list finishes loading.

---

## 10. Why can't I add Team Members?

Team members are selected from employees assigned to the current Business Location.

### Possible Causes

- No employees are available.
- Employees are already assigned where duplicate assignments are restricted.
- You do not have permission to manage teams.

### What You Can Do

- Create employee records first.
- Verify employee availability.
- Contact your administrator if additional permissions are required.

---

## 11. Why is the Employee Details page empty?

Employee information is displayed only for the selected Business Location.

### Possible Causes

- No employees have been assigned to the location.
- An incorrect Business Location is selected.
- Employee data is still loading.

### What You Can Do

- Select the correct Business Location.
- Refresh the page.
- Verify that employees have been assigned to the location.

---

## 12. Why am I getting a **403 Access Denied** or **Unauthorized** message?

Your account does not have permission to access the requested feature.

### Possible Causes

- Your assigned role does not include the required permissions.
- Your login session has expired.
- Access has been restricted by your administrator.

### What You Can Do

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Verify that you are using the correct account.
- Contact your administrator to request access.

---

## 13. Why am I seeing a **404 Page Not Found** error?

The requested page or resource could not be found.

### Possible Causes

- The page has been removed or renamed.
- The URL is incorrect.
- You do not have permission to access the resource.

### What You Can Do

- Return to the Business Hub dashboard.
- Refresh the page.
- Verify that the resource exists.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 14. Why am I seeing a **500 Internal Server Error**?

A 500 error indicates that the server encountered an unexpected problem.

### Possible Causes

- Temporary server outage.
- Database connection failure.
- Internal application error.
- Scheduled system maintenance.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Check your internet connection.
- Try again later.
- Contact your system administrator or support team if the problem persists.

---

## 15. Why are my changes not being saved?

Changes may not be saved if validation fails or your connection is interrupted.

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are missing.
- Invalid information was entered.
- Network interruption occurred.
- Your session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Verify the entered information.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- Sign in again if your session has expired.

---

## 16. Why isn't Search returning any results?

Search displays only records that match the entered keyword.

### Possible Causes

- The keyword is misspelled.
- No matching records exist.
- Active filters are limiting the results.

### What You Can Do

- Check the spelling.
- Search using part of the name.
- Clear any applied filters.
- Refresh the page and try again.

---

## 17. Why am I seeing a **Network Error**?

A Network Error means the application cannot communicate with the server.

### Possible Causes

- Internet connection has been lost.
- VPN or firewall restrictions.
- Temporary server outage.

### What You Can Do

- Verify your internet connection.
- Refresh the page.
- Disable VPN if applicable.
- Contact your IT administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 18. Why are some dashboard charts not displaying?

Dashboard charts are generated only when sufficient data is available.

### Possible Causes

- No project or employee data exists.
- Data synchronization is still in progress.
- Filters are limiting the displayed information.

### What You Can Do

- Verify that records have been created.
- Clear any active filters.
- Refresh the dashboard after a few moments.

---

## 19. Why can't I switch to another Business Unit?

Switching is available only for Business Units assigned to your account.

### Possible Causes

- You have access to only one Business Unit.
- Your role restricts access to other Business Units.
- Business Unit information is unavailable.

### What You Can Do

- Verify your assigned Business Units.
- Refresh the page.
- Contact your administrator to request access.

---

## 20. Why are Recent Activities or Upcoming Deadlines empty?

These widgets display information only when activities or deadlines exist.

### Possible Causes

- No recent activity has been recorded.
- No upcoming project deadlines are available.
- Filters are hiding the data.

### What You Can Do
