# FAQ

### 1. How do I add Cc or Bcc recipients when composing an email?

**Solution:** Inside the **New mail** modal, look directly beneath the **To** input box and click **Cc** or **Bcc**. Additional input fields will expand, allowing you to add carbon copy or blind carbon copy recipient addresses.

---

### 2. How do I edit or delete a custom Tag?

**Solution:** Hover your mouse cursor over the tag you want to change in the left sidebar. Click the three vertical dots (`⋮`) that appear on the right side, then choose **Edit** to update the tag or **Delete** to remove it.

---

### 3. What happens if I click the cancel (✕) button while creating a Tag?

Clicking `✕` cancels tag creation, discards any text typed into the tag input field, and closes the inline input row without making changes.

---

### 4. Why is the "Send" button disabled in the New Mail modal?

The **Send** button remains inactive until minimum email parameters are fulfilled.

**Possible Causes:**

- No recipient email address was added in the **To** field (you must press `Enter` after typing the email).
- The recipient email format is invalid.

**What You Can Do:**

- Type a valid email address into the **To** field and press `Enter` so it turns into a tag chip before sending.

---

### 5. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error when sending an email?

A 500 Internal Server Error indicates an unexpected backend failure while connecting to the mail server.

**Possible Causes:**

- Temporary outage of the outgoing SMTP mail service.
- Server timeout during attachment processing.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page after a few minutes and re-attempt sending your email.
- Save your email content externally before refreshing to prevent data loss.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue continues.

---

### 6. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied error on the Mail screen?

A 403 error indicates that your user account lacks permission to send or manage emails on this workspace.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your login session token has expired.
- Mail privileges have been restricted for your user role.

**What You Can Do:**

- Sign out and log back in to refresh your active session.
- Contact your IT administrator to confirm your mail account access rights.
