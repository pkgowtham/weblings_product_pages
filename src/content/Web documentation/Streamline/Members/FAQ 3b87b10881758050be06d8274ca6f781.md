# FAQ

### 1. Why can't I delete a member directly without selecting a replacement?

Direct deletion without replacement is restricted by system workflow rules to protect your project data.

**Explanation:**

- If a team member is deleted directly, any active tasks, stories, or epics assigned to them will become unassigned. This disrupts project workflows, distorts sprint tracking, and leaves active work unaccounted for. Selecting a replacement ensures all assigned work transfers seamlessly to another active team member.

---

### 2. Why is the "Replace & Remove" button disabled or grayed out?

The **Replace & Remove** button remains inactive until all required fields are completed inside the modal.

**Possible Causes:**

- You have not selected a replacement user from the **Select new member** dropdown.

**What You Can Do:**

- Click the **Select a member** dropdown menu inside the modal, choose an active employee from the list, and the **Replace & Remove** button will highlight blue and become clickable.

---

### 3. What happens to the departing member's assigned tasks after replacement?

When you click **Replace & Remove**:

- All tasks, epics, stories, and subtasks currently assigned to the departing employee across the specified project(s) are automatically reassigned to the selected replacement member.
- The original member is then removed from the organization's Members list.

---

### 4. Why am I unable to find a specific employee in the "Select new member" dropdown?

If a specific user does not appear in the replacement dropdown list:

**Possible Causes:**

- The employee has not been added to the organization directory yet.
- The employee is already the person being removed.
- Active search or filter restrictions are limiting the dropdown list.

**What You Can Do:**

- Ensure the replacement user is listed in the main **Members** table before attempting the replacement process.

---

### 5. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error when trying to replace a member?

A 500 Internal Server Error indicates an unexpected backend failure during the task transfer or member removal process.

**Possible Causes:**

- Temporary server outage while updating database issue assignees.
- Network timeout during bulk task reassignment.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page after a few minutes and re-attempt the replacement.
- Sign out and log back in to refresh your active session token.
- Contact your system administrator if the error continues.

---

### 6. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied error on the Members screen?

Member offboarding and reassignment require administrative privileges.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your account role does not have administrative permissions to modify user memberships.
- Your session token has expired.

**What You Can Do:**

- Sign out and log back in.
- Request *Administrator* or *HR Manager* access permissions from your system administrator.
