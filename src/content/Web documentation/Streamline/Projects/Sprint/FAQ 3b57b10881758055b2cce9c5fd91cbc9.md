# FAQ

### 1. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error while opening the Sprint page?

A 500 Internal Server Error usually indicates that the server encountered an unexpected problem while processing your request.

**Possible Causes:**

- The server is temporarily unavailable.
- The backend service responsible for loading sprint data is not responding.
- The database connection failed.
- The system is undergoing scheduled maintenance.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Check your internet connection.
- Contact your system administrator or support team if the issue continues.

---

### 2. Why can't I create a new sprint?

If clicking **Create** does not save the sprint, the input values may not have passed validation.

**Possible Causes:**

- The **Sprint Name** was left empty.
- Either the **Start Date** or **End Date** is missing.
- The Start Date is configured later than the End Date.
- Your user role does not have permission to create sprints.

**What You Can Do:**

- Enter a valid Sprint Name.
- Select both Start and End dates from the date pickers.
- Verify that the Start Date occurs before or on the End Date.
- Request administrative permissions if the issue persists.

---

### 3. Why are my sprint updates not saving when I click Update?

If modified dates or titles revert after clicking **Update**, communication with the server may have been interrupted.

**Possible Causes:**

- Network connectivity was temporarily lost during submission.
- Your login session has expired.
- Invalid date range inputs.

**What You Can Do:**

- Verify your network connection and try again.
- Refresh the page and log in again if your session timed out.
- Ensure all required date fields contain valid values.

---

### 4. Why are no tasks showing up inside my sprint?

If your sprint card states *"No tasks in this sprint"*, no items are currently assigned or visible.

**Possible Causes:**

- No tasks have been dragged or linked to this sprint from the Backlog.
- Active search bar entries or dropdown filters (Task Type, Priority, Status, Tags) are hiding assigned tasks.

**What You Can Do:**

- Navigate to the **Backlog** tab and drag tasks into this sprint.
- Reset all top filter dropdowns to **All** and clear the text search bar.

---

### 5. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied or Unauthorized message?

This message indicates that your user account does not have permission to manage sprints for this project.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your assigned user role is limited to view-only access.
- Your session token has expired.
- You are not explicitly added to the project (`swiggy project`).

**What You Can Do:**

- Sign out and sign back into your account.
- Verify you are accessing the correct user account.
- Contact your project administrator to grant sprint edit permissions.

---

### 6. Why is the Sprint list not loading?

If the sprint list remains blank or shows a loading spinner continuously, data retrieval failed.

**Possible Causes:**

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- Temporary network block caused by a VPN, proxy, or firewall.
- Server timeout while fetching project data.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page.
- Check your internet connection.
- Disable any active VPN or proxy tools and try again.
