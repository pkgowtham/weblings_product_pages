# FAQ

### 1. Why can't I drag and drop a task card to another column?

If a task card refuses to move or snaps back to its original column, one of the following issues may be preventing the update.

**Possible Causes:**

- You do not have permission to change task statuses in this project.
- Your internet connection was briefly interrupted while dragging.
- The task has mandatory required fields (such as Assignee or Due Date) that must be filled before moving to *In Progress* or *Done*.
- Your login session expired while the page was open.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page and try dragging the card again.
- Click on the task card to open its detail view and ensure all required metadata fields are completed.
- Check your user role permissions with your administrator.

---

### 2. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error on the Board screen?

A 500 Internal Server Error indicates an unexpected backend failure while loading board columns or updating task statuses.

**Possible Causes:**

- The server responsible for board services is temporarily offline.
- The database failed to fetch sprint items.
- Server overload or temporary system maintenance.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page after waiting a few minutes.
- Sign out of your account and log back in.
- Contact your system administrator or technical support team if the error persists.

---

### 3. Why are my board cards missing or not showing up?

If your board appears empty or is missing expected tasks, filters or sprint selections may be hiding them.

**Possible Causes:**

- The **Sprint Name** dropdown is set to a sprint that contains no tasks.
- Active filters (**Task Type**, **Priority**, **Status**, **Assignee**) are filtering out your tasks.
- Search text remains inside the `Search task...` bar.

**What You Can Do:**

- Check the **Sprint Name** filter dropdown and ensure the correct active sprint (e.g., *Casa Tower*) is selected.
- Reset all filter dropdowns (**Task Type**, **Priority**, **Status**, **Assignee**) back to **All**.
- Clear all text from the top search bar.

---

### 4. Why doesn't my newly created task appear in the column where I clicked "+ Create issue"?

If you clicked **+ Create issue** under *Onprocess*, but the saved card appeared under *TODO*, the modal's default status field overrode your selection.

**Possible Causes:**

- The **Status** field inside the Create modal was set to *TODO* or *NO STATUS* during creation.

**What You Can Do:**

- When filling out the Create modal, verify that the **Status** field matches your desired column state before clicking **Create**.
- Simply drag and drop the newly created card into the correct column on the board.

---

### 5. Why can't I see columns like "Hold" or "Done" without scrolling?

On smaller screen resolutions or zoomed-in displays, board columns extend horizontally off-screen.

**Possible Causes:**

- Horizontal board overflow due to screen size limits.

**What You Can Do:**

- Use the **horizontal scroll bar** located at the bottom of the board area to scroll right and view all columns.
- Adjust your browser zoom level (e.g., zoom out to 90% or 80%) to fit all columns on screen simultaneously.

---

### 6. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied error when trying to edit or move a card?

A 403 Access Denied message indicates that your assigned project permissions restrict modifying work items.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your account is configured with *Read-Only* or *Viewer* permissions for this sprint.
- Your session token expired while working on the page.

**What You Can Do:**

- Sign out and sign back into your account to refresh your token.
- Request *Contributor* or *Board Editor* permissions from your system administrator.

---

### 7. Why is the Board list taking a long time to load or showing a constant spinner?

If task cards take an unusually long time to render or display loading spinners, communication with the server is delayed.

**Possible Causes:**

- Slow or unstable local network connection.
- Active VPN, proxy, or firewall blocking WebSocket or API communication.
- High server load during peak hours.

**What You Can Do:**

- Verify your local internet connection.
- Temporarily disable any active VPN or proxy software.
- Refresh the page to re-establish the connection.

---

### 8. What happens if I click Cancel while creating a task on the Board?

Clicking **Cancel** immediately closes the creation form and discards all unsubmitted titles, descriptions, dates, priorities, and uploaded files. No draft is saved on the board.
