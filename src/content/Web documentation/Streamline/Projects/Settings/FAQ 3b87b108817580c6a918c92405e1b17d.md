# FAQ

### 1. Why can't I drag and drop items to reorder them in Priority, Task Type, or Tags?

Drag-and-drop reordering is **only supported on the Status tab**.

**Explanation:**

- **Status:** Requires manual reordering because it dictates the column order on the Kanban Board.
- **Priority, Task Type, and Tags:** These items use fixed system ordering or level hierarchies and cannot be manually rearranged.

---

### 2. Why am I unable to drag and drop items on the Status screen?

If status cards snap back or fail to move when dragged:

**Possible Causes:**

- You are not clicking directly on the drag handle area (`::`) on the left side of the row.
- Your account lacks administrator permissions to modify project workflow settings.
- Network delay prevented the updated order from saving to the server.

**What You Can Do:**

- Hover over the row and ensure you click and drag using the six-dot icon (`::`).
- Refresh the page and try reordering again.
- Contact your system administrator to verify you have project edit rights.

---

### 3. What happens to existing tasks if I delete a Status, Priority, or Task Type?

Deleting a configuration item affects active issues assigned to that metadata value.

**Possible Causes:**

- Tasks linked to a deleted status or type may lose their board column mapping or revert to a default fallback state (*NO STATUS* or *TODO*).

**What You Can Do:**

- Before deleting a status or priority, reassign all affected tasks on the Board or Backlog to an alternative active status or priority level.

---

### 4. Why is the "Create" or "Update" button disabled inside the settings modal?

If you cannot click **Create** or **Update** when adding/editing an item:

**Possible Causes:**

- A mandatory input field (such as Status Name, Priority Name, or Task Type Name) was left empty.
- An item with the exact same name already exists in your project settings.

**What You Can Do:**

- Fill out all required text fields inside the modal.
- Ensure the name you entered is unique and not a duplicate.

---

### 5. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error when saving settings?

A 500 error indicates an unexpected backend failure while updating project settings.

**Possible Causes:**

- Temporary server outage or database connection error while updating project configurations.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page after a few minutes and re-attempt the change.
- Sign out and log back in to refresh your active session token.
- Contact technical support if the error persists.

---

### 6. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied error on the Settings page?

Modifying project configurations requires administrative access.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your assigned role is set to *Viewer* or *Contributor* rather than *Project Administrator*.
- Your login session expired.

**What You Can Do:**

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Request *Project Administrator* privileges from your project owner.
