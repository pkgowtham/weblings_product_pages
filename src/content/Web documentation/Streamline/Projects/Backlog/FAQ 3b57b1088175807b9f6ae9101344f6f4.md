# FAQ

### 1. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error while opening the Backlog page?

A 500 Internal Server Error usually indicates that the server encountered an unexpected problem while processing your request.

**Possible Causes:**

- The server is temporarily unavailable.
- The backend service loading backlog and sprint data is not responding.
- The database connection failed.
- The application is currently under maintenance.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Check your internet connection.
- Contact your system administrator or support team if the problem continues.

---

### 2. Why can't I create a new sprint?

If clicking **Create** in the New Sprint modal does not work, the inputs may be incomplete.

**Possible Causes:**

- The **Sprint Name** was left blank.
- The **Start Date** or **End Date** was not selected.
- The Start Date is configured later than the End Date.
- Your account lacks sprint management permissions.

**What You Can Do:**

- Ensure the Sprint Name field is filled out.
- Select valid Start and End dates.
- Verify that the Start Date occurs before or on the End Date.
- Contact your system administrator if access permissions are required.

---

### 3. Why are no tasks showing up inside my sprint?

If your sprint shows *"No tasks in this sprint"*, no work items have been moved to it yet.

**Possible Causes:**

- Issues created in the backlog have not been dragged and dropped into the sprint container.
- Active search or filter options (Task Type, Priority, Status, Tags) are hiding assigned items.

**What You Can Do:**

- Drag and drop issues from the **Backlogs** panel on the left directly into the **Sprint** container on the right.
- Reset all top filter dropdowns to **All** and clear text from the search bar.

---

### 4. Why am I unable to add or create a new tag?

Tags must meet system validation rules before being added.

**Possible Causes:**

- The tag name field is empty.
- A tag with the exact same name already exists.
- Network delay prevented the server from saving the custom tag.

**What You Can Do:**

- Enter a unique tag name in the tag creation input field.
- Ensure you click the confirmation checkmark or **Okay** button after defining the tag.
- Refresh the page if the new tag does not appear immediately.

---

### 5. Why am I seeing a 403 Access Denied or Unauthorized message?

This message indicates that your user role does not have administrative privileges to modify backlogs or sprints.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your assigned role is set to view-only.
- Your login session has expired.
- You are not explicitly assigned to the selected project (`swiggy project`).

**What You Can Do:**

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Ensure you are using the correct user account.
- Request administrative permissions for sprints and backlogs from your system administrator.
