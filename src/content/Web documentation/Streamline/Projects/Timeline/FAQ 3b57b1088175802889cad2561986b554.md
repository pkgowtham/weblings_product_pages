# FAQ

### 1. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error while opening the Timeline page?

A 500 Internal Server Error usually indicates that the server encountered an unexpected problem while processing your request.

**Possible Causes:**

- The server is temporarily unavailable.
- The backend service loading project schedule data is not responding.
- The database connection failed.
- The system is undergoing scheduled maintenance.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Check your internet connection.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue persists.

---

### 2. Why isn't my created task appearing on the timeline grid?

If clicking **Create** adds the task to the list but not on the timeline chart, date parameters may be missing.

**Possible Causes:**

- Either the **Start Date** or **End Date** was left blank.
- The item is hidden inside a collapsed Epic.
- Search filters are hiding the item.

**What You Can Do:**

- Ensure both **Start Date** and **End Date** are set.
- Expand the parent **Epic** by clicking the drop-down arrow next to it.
- Clear any text in the **Search Timeline** bar.

---

### 3. Why can't I save or update task timings on the timeline?

The system validates schedule data before saving items.

**Possible Causes:**

- The **Start Date** is set later than the **End Date**.
- Required fields (like Status or Priority) were left blank.
- Invalid file format in the Attachment section.

**What You Can Do:**

- Verify that the Start Date occurs before or on the End Date.
- Fill out all mandatory fields before clicking **Create** or **Task Update**.
- Check file upload size and format constraints.

---

### 4. Why are my changes on the timeline not saving?

If task updates or date adjustments revert, communication with the server may have been interrupted.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your internet connection was briefly interrupted.
- Your login session expired.
- The server rejected the update request.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page and try again.
- Sign in again if your session has expired.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue continues.

---

### 5. Why can't I delete an Epic or Task?

Certain items cannot be deleted if active subtasks or dependent linkages exist.

**Possible Causes:**

- The Epic contains active Stories, Tasks, or Subtasks.
- The task is linked as a dependency to another active task.
- Your role does not have permission to delete project items.

**What You Can Do:**

- Reassign or delete child subtasks/stories before deleting the main Epic.
- Remove linked task associations.
- Contact your administrator to request additional permissions.

---

### 6. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied or Unauthorized message?

This message indicates that your account does not have permission to access or modify this project timeline.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your assigned role does not include project modification permissions.
- Your login session has expired.
- You are not assigned to this specific project.

**What You Can Do:**

- Sign out and sign back in.
- Ensure you are logged into the correct user account.
- Contact your system administrator to request project access.

---

### 7. Why is the Timeline list not loading?

If the timeline chart remains empty or shows a constant loading spinner, data retrieval may have failed.

**Possible Causes:**

- Slow or unstable internet connection.
- No tasks or epics have been created for this project yet.
- Server request timeout.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page.
- Check your network connection.
- Create a new task to populate the timeline.

---

### 8. Why can't I find a task using Search?

If the search feature returns no results, the query may not match existing item titles.

**Possible Causes:**

- The task title was entered incorrectly.
- You are looking in the wrong project directory.
- The task has been deleted.

**What You Can Do:**

- Verify spelling in the **Search Timeline** field.
- Confirm you are on the right project navigation path.
- Clear the search bar to show all timeline items.

---

### 9. Why am I seeing a Network Error message?

A Network Error indicates the web app cannot communicate with the system server.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your internet connection is unstable.
- The server is temporarily offline.
- A VPN, proxy, or firewall is blocking the connection.

**What You Can Do:**

- Verify your local network connection.
- Temporarily disable VPN or proxy if enabled.
- Refresh the page and try again.

---

### 10. Why was I automatically logged out while creating or updating a task?

For security reasons, inactive user sessions expire automatically after a set period.

**Possible Causes:**

- The modal was left open without interaction for too long.
- You signed in from another tab or browser device.

**What You Can Do:**

- Sign back into your account.
- Save work regularly when configuring complex epics or tasks.
