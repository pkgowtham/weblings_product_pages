# FAQ

### 1. Why is an invited user not showing up in the main Members table?

When an invitation is sent, the user is not added to the active Members list immediately.

**Possible Causes:**

- The user has not accepted the email invitation yet.
- The invitation email went to the user's spam or junk folder.

**What You Can Do:**

- Check the **Invited** section at the top of the Members page to verify the invite status.
- Click **Resend all invites** to send the invitation email again.
- Ask the user to check their email inbox (and spam folder) and click the acceptance link.

---

### 2. Why can't I click the "Add Member" button inside the modal?

If the **Add Member** button remains disabled or grayed out, mandatory fields are incomplete.

**Possible Causes:**

- You have not selected a user in the **Select Member** dropdown.
- You have not selected a team in the **Select Team** dropdown.

**What You Can Do:**

- Ensure both **Select Member** and **Select Team** fields are filled out before clicking **Add Member**.

---

### 3. Why am I unable to send an email invitation?

If clicking **Send Invites** does not dispatch the email, input validation may have failed.

**Possible Causes:**

- No email address was added to the tag box (you must press `Enter` or click `➤` after typing the email).
- The email address format is invalid.
- No team was selected in the **Select Team** dropdown.

**What You Can Do:**

- Select a team from the dropdown.
- Type the full email address and press `Enter` so it turns into a blue tag chip before clicking **Send Invites**.

---

### 4. Why must I select a replacement member when removing someone?

To prevent orphaned tasks, unassigned issues, or broken workflow dependencies, the system requires reassigning existing workloads.

**Possible Causes:**

- System workflow policy requires all active items assigned to the departing user to be reassigned upon removal.

**What You Can Do:**

- Select a team member from the **Select new member** dropdown inside the Replace Member modal, then click **Replace & Remove**.

---

### 5. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error on the Members page?

A 500 Internal Server Error indicates an unexpected problem on the server while managing team membership.

**Possible Causes:**

- The user directory or email notification service is temporarily down.
- Database failure while updating user roles.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Contact your system administrator if the issue continues.

---

### 6. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied error when trying to add or remove members?

Managing team members requires administrative permissions.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your account role does not have permission to invite, add, or remove project members.
- Your session expired.

**What You Can Do:**

- Sign out and sign back in to refresh your login session.
- Request *Project Administrator* or *Team Manager* permissions from your system administrator.
