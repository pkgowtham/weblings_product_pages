# FAQ

### 1. How do I add a new Tag under Settings?

**Solution:** Go to **Streamline > Settings > Tags**, click **New Tags**, type your tag name into the input box, pick a badge color by clicking the color dot, and click the checkmark (`✓`) to save.

---

### 2. What happens if I click the cancel button (✕) while creating a tag?

Clicking `✕` discards any text typed into the tag box and closes the inline input row without creating the tag.

---

### 3. Can I rearrange Tags using drag and drop?

No. Unlike Statuses, **Tags cannot be rearranged using drag and drop**. Tags are listed in order of creation or alphabetically.

---

### 4. What happens to existing project tasks if I delete a Tag, Status, or Priority?

Deleting an item removes it as an option from future project creation forms.

**Explanation:**

- Before deleting a tag, status, or priority, ensure no active tasks are currently relying on that label. If tasks are assigned to a deleted tag, they may lose their tag association or revert to an unassigned state.

---

### 5. Why can't I see the edit or delete option on a Tag or Status?

Action menus are hidden by default to keep the interface clean.

**Solution:**

- Hover your mouse cursor over the specific row you wish to modify. The three-dot menu icon (`⋮`) will appear on the right side. Click `⋮` to reveal **Edit** and **Delete** options.

---

### 6. Why is the checkmark (✓) button disabled when creating a tag?

If the save checkmark is grayed out or non-clickable:

**Possible Causes:**

- The tag name field is empty.
- A tag with the exact same name already exists in your settings.

**What You Can Do:**

- Enter a unique tag name into the text box to enable the checkmark button.

---

### 7. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied message on the Settings page?

Modifying global organization settings requires elevated access rights.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your user role does not have administrative permissions for global Streamline settings.
- Your login session has expired.

**What You Can Do:**

- Sign out and sign back in to refresh your token.
- Request *Administrator* privileges from your system owner.
