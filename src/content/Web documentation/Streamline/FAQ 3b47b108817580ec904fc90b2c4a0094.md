# FAQ

## 1. Why can't I create a project?

### Possible Causes

- Required fields are incomplete.
- Project Name already exists.
- No Project Manager has been selected.
- You do not have permission to create projects.

### What You Can Do

- Complete all required fields.
- Enter a unique project name.
- Select a Project Manager.
- Contact your administrator if the issue continues.

---

## 2. Why can't I add project members?

### Possible Causes

- No employees are available.
- Employees are inactive.
- You do not have permission to manage members.

### What You Can Do

- Verify employee availability.
- Refresh the page.
- Contact your administrator.

---

## 3. Why can't I edit a project?

### Possible Causes

- You do not have edit permission.
- The project is locked.
- Your session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Sign in again.
- Verify your permissions.
- Contact your administrator.

---

## 4. Why can't I delete a project?

### Possible Causes

- The project contains active tasks.
- An active sprint exists.
- You do not have permission to delete projects.

### What You Can Do

- Complete or reassign active work.
- Close active sprints.
- Contact your administrator if deletion is required.

---

## 5. Why isn't the project appearing in the list?

### Possible Causes

- The project has not been created successfully.
- Search filters are applied.
- Temporary synchronization delay.

### What You Can Do

- Refresh the page.
- Clear search filters.
- Verify that the project was created successfully.

---

## 6. Why can't I access the Project Details page?

### Possible Causes

- You do not have permission to view the project.
- The project has been deleted.
- Your session has expired.

### What You Can Do

- Sign in again.
- Verify your access permissions.
- Contact your administrator.
