# FAQ

### 1. Why am I unable to attach or send my files in chat?

The chat system enforces attachment limits to ensure optimal performance.

**Possible Causes:**

- You are attempting to attach more than **10 files at a time**.
- The file size exceeds the maximum permitted attachment threshold.
- Unstable internet connection interrupted the upload.

**What You Can Do:**

- Reduce your selection to a maximum of 10 files per message batch.
- Check your network connection and try re-uploading.

---

### 2. Why can't I click "Continue" or "Create Team" when creating a group?

The creation wizard requires minimum information before saving.

**Possible Causes:**

- The **Group Name** field was left empty in Step 1.
- No team members were selected in Step 2.

**What You Can Do:**

- Enter a group name before clicking **Continue**.
- Select at least one member from the member list before clicking **Create Team**.

---

### 3. Why are my theme or role settings changes not taking effect?

Customizations must be explicitly saved.

**Possible Causes:**

- You navigated away from the settings screen without clicking **Save Changes**.

**What You Can Do:**

- Adjust your preferred mode or accent color, then ensure you click the **Save Changes** button at the bottom of the screen.

---

### 4. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error when sending messages or making calls?

A 500 error indicates a temporary backend service disruption.

**Possible Causes:**

- WebSockets or real-time call servers are temporarily offline.
- Server timeout during message delivery.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page after a few minutes.
- Sign out and log back in.
- Contact your IT administrator if audio/video calls or messaging continue to fail.

---

### 5. Why am I receiving a 403 Access Denied error when editing group info or removing members?

Only authorized group administrators can alter channel settings.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your account is a standard group member without administrative or moderation rights.

**What You Can Do:**

- Ask the group creator or an administrator to grant you administrative privileges or make the requested changes on your behalf.
