# Mail

![mail (3).png](Mail/mail_(3).png)

The Mail module provides a full-featured email client to manage your inbox, compose new messages, organize emails using folders and custom tags, and respond to incoming communications.

The interface is divided into three main panels:

- **Sidebar Navigation (Left):** Access Mailboxes, Labels, and custom Tags.
- **Email List Panel (Middle):** View incoming or sent messages, complete with search, selection, and bulk action controls.
- **Email Reading Pane (Right):** Read the selected email, view attachments, and access quick **Reply** or **Forward** options.

---

## 1. Mailboxes and Labels Navigation

Use the left sidebar to navigate through your organized folders and categories:

- **Mailboxes:**
    - **Inbox:** View all incoming messages.
    - **Sent:** View emails you have dispatched.
    - **Spam:** View filtered marketing or unverified emails.
    - **Trash:** View deleted messages.
- **Labels:**
    - **Archive:** View archived email records.
    - **Important:** Access high-priority emails flagged as important.

---

## 2. Managing Custom Tags

Tags allow you to color-code and organize emails by topic or project.

### Creating a New Tag

![Edit (1).png](Mail/Edit_(1).png)

1. In the left sidebar, locate the **Tag** section and click the **`+`** icon.
2. An inline input row will appear:
    - **Tag Name Input:** Type the name of your tag.
    - **Color Circle:** Click the color dot to select a custom color indicator.
3. Click the checkmark button (`✓`) to save the tag.
4. Click the cancel button (`✕`) to discard without saving.

### Editing or Deleting a Tag

![Edit (2).png](Mail/Edit_(2).png)

1. Hover your cursor over any existing tag item in the left sidebar.
2. Click the three vertical dots (`⋮`) that appear on the right side of the tag row.
3. Select your desired action:
    - **Edit:** Opens the inline editor to update the tag name or color.
    - **Delete:** Permanently removes the tag.

---

## 3. Composing a New Email

![mail (5).png](Mail/mail_(5).png)

To compose and send an email:

1. Click the blue **compose** button at the top of the left sidebar.
2. The **New mail** modal will open:
    - **To:** Type the recipient's email address (e.g., `example@gmail.com`) and press `Enter`.
    - **Cc / Bcc:** Click the **Cc** or **Bcc** links below the recipient box to expand additional Carbon Copy (Cc) or Blind Carbon Copy (Bcc) address fields.
    - **Subject:** Enter the title or subject line of your message.
    - **Compose:** Type your main email body text.
    - **Attachment:** Click the paperclip icon (`📎`) at the bottom to attach files or documents.
3. Click the **Send ➤** button to dispatch your email.

---

## 4. Reading and Replying to Emails

When you select an email from the message list, it renders in the right reading pane:

- **View Attachments:** Download or view files (e.g., `Document.pdf`) attached to the email.
- **Reply:** Click **Reply** at the bottom of the email pane to send a direct response to the original sender.
- **Forward:** Click **Forward** to send a copy of the email thread and its attachments to a new recipient.
