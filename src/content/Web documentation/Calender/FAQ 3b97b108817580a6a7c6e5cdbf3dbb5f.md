# FAQ

### 1. How do multi-day tasks appear on the Calendar?

Multi-day tasks span continuously across the calendar grid.

**Explanation:**

- A task scheduled across multiple days (e.g., from June 11 to June 17) appears as a continuous horizontal colored bar stretching across those date cells on the Month view.

---

### 2. Why am I unable to find a specific task or meeting on my Calendar?

If an expected event does not appear on your view, filter settings or view modes may be hiding it.

**Possible Causes:**

- Active **Tag**, **Event**, or **Assignee** filters are excluding the item.
- You are currently viewing the wrong month or date range.

**What You Can Do:**

- Clear all selections inside the **Tag**, **Event**, and **Assignee** filter dropdowns.
- Click the **Today** button or use the navigation arrows (`<` / `>`) to return to the month where the event was scheduled.

---

### 3. What is the difference between creating a "Task" and a "Meet" item?

The calendar accommodates both work items and collaborative schedules.

**Explanation:**

- **Task:** Focuses on work deliverables, linking start/end date ranges to specific projects and tags.
- **Meet:** Focuses on timed calendar events, capturing specific start/end times, attendee list tags (*People*), and agenda attachments.

---

### 4. Why is the "Create" button disabled inside the creation modal?

The confirmation button remains inactive until all required scheduling metadata is provided.

**Possible Causes:**

- Mandatory fields (such as Title, Project, or From/To dates) were left empty.
- The "From" date is configured later than the "To" date.

**What You Can Do:**

- Ensure all text fields and dropdowns are filled out correctly.
- Verify that your Start Date occurs on or before your End Date.

---

### 5. Why am I seeing a 500 Internal Server Error on the Calendar page?

A 500 error indicates a server failure while retrieving or saving schedule data.

**Possible Causes:**

- Temporary server connection timeout or database error while fetching calendar entries.

**What You Can Do:**

- Refresh the page after waiting a few minutes.
- Sign out and log back in to refresh your user session.
- Contact technical support if the issue persists.

---

### 6. Why am I getting a 403 Access Denied error when trying to create an event?

Creating or modifying calendar events requires active user authorization.

**Possible Causes:**

- Your assigned role has view-only permissions for the selected project calendar.
- Your session token has expired.

**What You Can Do:**

- Sign out and log back in.
- Request event creation permissions from your project administrator.
