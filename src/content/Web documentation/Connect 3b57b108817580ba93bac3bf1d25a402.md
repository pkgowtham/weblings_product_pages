# Connect

![chat-Create group (3).png](Connect/chat-Create_group_(3).png)

The Connect module provides real-time collaboration tools, enabling direct individual messaging, group discussions, audio/video calls, file sharing, and workspace settings customization.

---

## 1. Navigating the Chats Interface

The main messaging view is organized into a sidebar list and an active conversation panel:

- **Filter Tabs:** Organize your conversation list by toggling between:
    - **All:** Displays all direct messages and group conversations.
    - **People:** Displays individual 1-on-1 direct message threads.
    - **Groups:** Displays team and project group channels.
- **Search Bar:** Type inside `Search for Messages, people, groups` to locate specific chat threads or content.
- **Call Header:** Use the video camera (`📹`) or telephone (`📞`) icons in the top-right header to initiate video or audio calls.
- **Media & Files View:** Switch to the **Files** tab to view shared documents, images, and attachments across conversations.

---

## 2. Creating a New Group

![chat-Create group (4).png](Connect/chat-Create_group_(4).png)

To set up a new group chat channel:

### Step 1: Set Group Profile & Info

1. Click the **Add Group / Member** icon (`👥+`) at the top of the chat list.
2. The **Create Group** modal will open:
    - **Group Icon:** Click **Click here to upload Group icon** to select a profile picture for the group.
    - **Group Name:** Enter a title for the group channel.
    - **Description:** Add optional details or the purpose of the group.
3. Click **Continue**.

### Step 2: Add Members & Finalize Creation

![chat-Create group (5).png](Connect/chat-Create_group_(5).png)

1. Use the search bar or browse the member directory to add team members.
2. Check or select members to add them to the selection panel (*Selected X*).
3. Click **Create Team** (or **Create Group**).
4. Once created, the new group will appear in your **Groups** chat list, ready for messaging.

## 3. Messaging, Attachments & Forwarding

### Sending Messages & Files

![Send Message (5).png](Connect/Send_Message_(5).png)

- Type your message into the bottom input field and press `Enter` or click the send button (`➤`).
- Click the **Attachment** paperclip icon (`📎`) to send files or photos. You can upload up to **10 files at a time** (Images, Videos, or Documents).

### Forwarding Messages

![Send Message (6).png](Connect/Send_Message_(6).png)

1. Hover over or click the options icon (`⋮`) next to a message bubble.
2. Select **Forward**.
3. The **Forward message to** modal will appear:
    - Search or select target members/groups by checking their selection boxes.
    - Review selected recipients in the bottom preview pane.
4. Click the blue **Forward** button to send the message to the selected contacts.

---

## 4. Group Info & Member Management

Clicking the three vertical dots (`⋮`) in the top-right header of an active group chat opens **Group Info**:

- **Edit Group Info:** Modify the group name, description, or group icon.
- **Manage Members:**
    - Click **Add Member** to invite additional people to the group.
    - Remove existing members or assign group moderator privileges.

---

## 5. Settings & Theme Customization

Access the Settings tab within Connect to adjust user preferences:

- **Role Permissions:** Manage messaging capabilities, member invite rights, and group creation permissions.
- **Theme Settings:**
    - **Mode:** Switch between *Light Mode* and *Dark Mode*.
    - **Accent Color:** Select a primary color theme for chat highlights and buttons.
- Click **Save Changes** to apply your updated theme and role settings.
