Beta Phase Details

What happens when the Beta ends in January?

In late December, we will notify all active beta organizations. You will have the choice to enter your payment details to transition into the $7 or $15 paid production tiers. If you choose not to upgrade, your account will be gracefully suspended, and you can export all your data freely. And if you bought a custom domain through us? No hostage situations. We will seamlessly transfer your domain to whichever platform you prefer, no questions asked.

Is my data secure during the Beta phase?

Yes. Even in beta, your data is housed in isolated databases protected by enterprise-grade encryption. Here is our absolute promise to you: we will never sell your data, we will never serve you advertisements in any form, and we will never use your private workspace data for anything other than running your software. Your project details are strictly yours.
